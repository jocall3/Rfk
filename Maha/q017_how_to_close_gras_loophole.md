## The Target: Section 201(s) and Section 409 of the FD&C Act

To understand how we blow this up, we have to look at the plumbing of the **Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act)**. 

Currently, **21 U.S.C. § 321(s)** defines a "food additive" but carves out a massive, truck-sized exemption:

> "...except that such term does not include any substance if it is generally recognized, among experts qualified by scientific training and experience to evaluate its safety... as having been adequately shown through scientific procedures... to be safe under the conditions of its intended use..."

This is the legal crack in the foundation. If a substance is "GRAS," it is legally *not* a food additive, which means it bypasses the pre-market approval process under **Section 409 (21 U.S.C. § 348)**. 

Here is the exact, surgical statutory draft we are handing to Bobby to rewrite the law.

## The Statutory Amendment: The GRAS Extermination Act of 2025

We are going to strike the GRAS exemption from the United States Code entirely. No more self-certification. No more secret panels. No more Garys.

### Amendment 1: Amending 21 U.S.C. § 321(s) (The Definition of Food Additive)

We strike the exemption language entirely. 

```text
Section 201(s) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 321(s)) is amended—
(1) by striking ", except that such term does not include any substance if it is generally recognized, among experts qualified by scientific training and experience to evaluate its safety, as having been adequately shown through scientific procedures (or, in the case of a substance used in food prior to January 1, 1958, through either scientific procedures or experience based on common use in food) to be safe under the conditions of its intended use"; and
(2) by striking paragraphs (1) through (6) and inserting a period.
```

**What this does:** It makes *every single chemical, preservative, emulsifier, artificial color, and synthetic flavoring agent* added to food legally classified as a **Food Additive**. No exceptions. No grandfathering. If it wasn't there before 1958 as a whole, raw food, you have to prove it's safe.

### Amendment 2: Overhauling Section 409 (21 U.S.C. § 348) to Mandate Formal Petitions

Now that everything is a food additive, we rewrite Section 409 to establish a mandatory, transparent, and adversarial **Food Additive Petition (FAP)** process.

```text
Section 409 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 348) is amended by adding the following new subsections:

"(l) MANDATORY PRE-MARKET PETITION AND APPROVAL REQUIRED.—
    (1) IN GENERAL.—No chemical substance, synthetic compound, or additive may be introduced into interstate commerce for use in or on food unless the sponsor of such substance has submitted to the Secretary a formal Food Additive Petition under subsection (b) and the Secretary has issued a final regulation approving such use.
    (2) PROHIBITION ON SELF-CERTIFICATION.—Any determination by a sponsor, manufacturer, or third-party panel that a substance is safe, independent of a final regulation issued by the Secretary under this section, shall have no legal effect and shall not exempt the substance from the requirements of this Act.
    (3) PUBLIC REGISTRY.—The Secretary shall establish and maintain a publicly accessible, searchable online database containing:
        (A) Every petition submitted under this section;
        (B) All raw toxicological, metabolic, and safety data submitted by the sponsor;
        (C) The names, affiliations, and funding sources of all experts involved in the safety evaluation; and
        (D) A transcript of all communications between the sponsor and the Food and Drug Administration.
    (4) BURDEN OF PROOF.—The burden of proving, by clear and convincing scientific evidence, that a substance is safe under its intended conditions of use shall rest solely on the sponsor. Safety evaluations must account for cumulative dietary exposure, endocrine disruption, and impacts on the human microbiome."
```

**What this does:** 
1. **Kills Self-Certification:** It explicitly states that "self-certification" has zero legal standing. If you try it, you go to jail.
2. **Forces Transparency:** No more secret science. All raw data must be uploaded to a public database. Independent scientists (and angry moms on Twitter) can tear your crappy corporate studies apart in real-time.
3. **Shifts the Burden of Proof:** The FDA doesn't have to prove a chemical is dangerous; *you* have to prove it is 100% safe, including what it does to your gut bacteria and your hormones.
