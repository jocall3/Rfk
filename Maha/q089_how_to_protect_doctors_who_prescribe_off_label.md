(a) IN GENERAL.—No state medical board, licensing authority, or hospital credentialing committee shall suspend, revoke, restrict, or take adverse administrative action against the license or privileges of a healthcare practitioner solely on the basis that the practitioner prescribed, dispensed, or administered an FDA-approved drug for an off-label indication, provided that:
