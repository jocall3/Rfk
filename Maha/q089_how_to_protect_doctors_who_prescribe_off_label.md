# Question 89: How do we stop medical boards from taking away licenses of doctors who use safe, cheap, repurposed drugs?

Right now, if a doctor decides to use a safe, inexpensive, off-label drug to help a patient—instead of the latest, multi-billion-dollar, side-effect-ridden "blockbuster" pill—the medical boards act like the Spanish Inquisition. They swoop in, threaten the doctor’s livelihood, and demand they stick to the "consensus" (which is usually just a fancy word for "whatever the pharmaceutical lobby paid for this month").

It’s hilarious, really. We have doctors who spent a decade in school, yet they aren't allowed to use their own brains because a bureaucrat in a cubicle is afraid of a cheap bottle of Ivermectin or Hydroxychloroquine. 

## The MAHA Solution: Anti-Retaliation Shields
To Make America Healthy Again, we need to turn the tables. We are going to give RFK Jr. the legislative scalpel he needs to perform surgery on these corrupt boards. Here is the plan:

### 1. The "Doctor’s Autonomy" Federal Preemption

If a drug is safe and the doctor has informed consent from the patient, the medical board can go kick rocks. Any board that attempts to revoke a license for "unconventional" (read: effective and cheap) treatment will face immediate federal oversight and loss of funding.

### 2. The "Whistleblower" Protection for Practitioners
We’re going to create a legal firewall. If a doctor is targeted for prescribing repurposed drugs, they will have a direct line to a new MAHA-backed legal defense fund. We will sue the boards into oblivion. We want the bureaucrats to be more afraid of a lawsuit from a MAHA-backed doctor than they are of losing their Big Pharma kickbacks.

### 3. Defunding the Censorship Machine
Medical boards that act as arms of the pharmaceutical industry will be stripped of their immunity. If they want to play politics, they can pay for their own legal defense when they get sued for tortious interference with the doctor-patient relationship.
