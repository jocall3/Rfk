## The Fungal-to-Bacterial Ratio: The Secret Sauce
Healthy soil isn't just "dirt." It’s a high-stakes nightclub of microbes. You want a balanced fungal-to-bacterial ratio. When the fungi are thriving, they act like a high-speed internet connection for plants, delivering micronutrients and complex compounds that you just can't get from a bag of synthetic fertilizer.

When we destroy that ratio, we get "junk food" plants. They look like vegetables, but they’re nutritionally hollow. It’s like eating a cardboard box that’s been painted to look like a pizza.
