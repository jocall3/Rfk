## The MAHA Solution: The National Public Health Modeling Registry (NPHMR)

We are giving Bobby (Robert F. Kennedy Jr.) the legislative and technological sledgehammer needed to dismantle secret government modeling forever. 

### 1. The Open Source Mandate ("The Git Push Rule")
Before any federal agency (CDC, NIH, HHS, FDA, DHS) or federally funded academic institution can present a predictive model to justify public health guidance, regulations, or emergency measures:
* **100% Code Transparency:** Every line of code, build script, docker container, and dependency must be uploaded to a public repository (e.g., `github.com/MAHA-OpenHealth`).
* **Open Data Standard:** Datasets used to train or parameterize the model must be published under anonymized, open-access formats. If the data cannot be shared due to privacy, a mathematically identical synthetic dataset must be provided so third parties can test code execution.
* **Deterministic Reproducibility:** Anyone in America—from a Harvard biostatistician to a 14-year-old kid in Ohio who loves Python—must be able to clone the repo, run `docker-compose up`, and yield the *exact* same results.

### 2. Mandatory Red-Teaming & Adversarial Peer Review
Instead of "peer review" consisting of three friends in the same grant-funding syndicate emailing each other "Looks good, Dave!", public health models will undergo **Adversarial Peer Review**:
* **Bounty Programs:** The federal government will issue bug bounties for independent programmers, data scientists, and statisticians who find logical flaws, overfitting, or code errors in proposed public health models.
* **Red Team Audits:** Before any model-driven policy is enacted, an independent panel of skeptics—including computer engineers, open-source maintainers, and field epidemiologists—will have 14 days to stress-test the model's assumptions.

### 3. Real-Time Performance Tracking & Model Sunsetting
Models will be judged like weather forecasts. If a hurricane model predicts Miami will be underwater by Tuesday and Tuesday comes and it's sunny, we calibrate the model. If a public health model predicts apocalyptic casualties unless we lock down the state of Florida, and Florida stays open and outperforms the locked-down states in overall wellbeing, **that model is immediately decommissioned and its authors are barred from federal modeling contracts.**
