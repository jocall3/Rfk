## Tactical Execution Plan: The OMB & HHS Inspector General Audit

To achieve an immediate, ironclad **20% administrative cost reduction**, HHS will execute the following four-stage strategy:

```
[Phase 1: Freeze & Inventory] ──> [Phase 2: Redundancy Mapping] ──> [Phase 3: The 20% Cut Directive] ──> [Phase 4: Reinvestment in MAHA Priorities]
```

### 1. Joint OMB-OIG Performance Audit Directive
Within 30 days of inauguration, the HHS Secretary will issue a joint directive with the Director of the OMB and the HHS Inspector General to initiate a top-to-bottom forensic management audit.
* **Scope:** Target the Office of the Secretary (OS), Assistant Secretary for Administration (ASA), and administrative support units within FDA, CDC, and NIH.
* **Focus Area:** Executive leadership overhead, travel budgets, third-party management consulting contracts, and duplicative communications/outreach teams.

### 2. The "30-Day Proof of Work" Rule
Every middle-management position above General Schedule (GS) Grade 13 without direct public-facing clinical, scientific research, or enforcement duties must submit a 1-page summary of their tangible contributions to public health outcomes over the previous 24 months.
* **The Glitch:** If your primary duty is "facilitating cross-agency dialogue regarding inter-departmental synergies," your position is officially classified as redundant.
* **Contractor Elimination:** Terminate all external management consulting contracts (looking at you, Deloitte and McKinsey) that advise the government on how to hire more government workers.

### 3. Merging Duplicative Functional Silos
HHS currently maintains dozens of separate "Office of Public Affairs," "Office of Legislative Liaison," and "Office of Diversity & Inclusion" units within *each individual sub-agency* (CDC, FDA, NIH, HRSA, SAMHSA).
* **Consolidation:** Collapse all sub-agency administrative, legal, communications, and HR operations into unified HHS-wide shared services. 
* **Savings:** Instantly cuts hundreds of middle-tier administrative directors, deputy directors, and assistant-to-the-deputy directors.
