Right now, Big Pharma is treating AI like a digital sales rep that never sleeps. They are training diagnostic models on proprietary, black-box datasets designed to do one thing: identify "symptoms" that just happen to be cured by their most expensive, side-effect-heavy patents. It’s not healthcare; it’s a subscription model for your biology. 

If we let them keep the keys to the kingdom, your AI doctor will be nothing more than a glorified vending machine for shareholders. RFK Jr. knows that if we want to Make America Healthy Again, we have to stop the algorithm from being a corporate shill.

## The Solution: The "Open-Source or Bust" Mandate

To ensure AI serves the patient and not the profit margin, we are implementing a three-pillar regulatory framework:

### 1. Mandatory Open-Source Transparency
Any AI diagnostic tool used in a clinical setting must have its source code and training weights audited by an independent, non-conflicted body. If the code is hidden behind a "proprietary" wall, it’s illegal. We want to see the math. If the algorithm is nudging patients toward a specific drug, we need to know if it’s because the drug works or because the drug company paid for the "nudge."

### 2. Absolute Biometric Sovereignty
Your health data is not a commodity. We are establishing a "Digital HIPAA" that mandates:
*   **Local Processing:** Diagnostic AI must run on local hardware (your phone or a local clinic server) whenever possible, not in a Big Tech cloud where it can be scraped and sold.
*   **Zero-Knowledge Proofs:** AI tools must be able to diagnose without ever "knowing" who you are. Your identity and your biology should be cryptographically decoupled.

### 3. The "Anti-Corruption" Audit
We are creating a federal task force—the "Algorithm Integrity Bureau"—to stress-test AI models against "Pharma-Bias." If an AI consistently ignores lifestyle interventions (like nutrition, sunlight, and movement) in favor of pharmaceutical interventions, the model will be flagged, fined, and forced to undergo a "re-education" of its training data.
