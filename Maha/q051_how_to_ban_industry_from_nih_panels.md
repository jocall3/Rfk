# MEMORANDUM FOR THE PRESIDENT

**TO:** President Donald J. Trump  
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc.  
**SUBJECT:** Operation "Surgical Strike" on NIH Corporate Parasites  

Mr. President, the NIH grant review boards are currently more infested with corporate shills than a New York City subway station is with rats, and it is time to fumigate the building [1]. We have identified that industry-linked reviewers are effectively acting as gatekeepers for their own profit margins, a conflict of interest so blatant it makes the 2008 financial crisis look like a lemonade stand [2]. To Make America Healthy Again (MAHA), we must implement a "Zero-Industry-Influence" policy, because asking a pharmaceutical executive to judge public health research is like asking a shark to chair a committee on lifeguard safety [3]. 

### 1. The "Clean Hands" Purge
We are instituting an immediate, non-negotiable ban on any individual serving on an NIH review board if they have held a position at a pharmaceutical, food, or chemical corporation within the last 10 years [4]. If you have spent the last decade peddling ultra-processed sludge or patent-protected pills, your opinion on "valid science" is worth less than a subprime mortgage bond [5]. 

### 2. The "Follow the Money" AI Audit
We are deploying an aggressive, AI-driven financial audit of every panel member to ensure they aren't hiding assets in offshore accounts or industry-funded "consulting" retainers [6]. If a reviewer owns stock in a company that benefits from the research they are reviewing, they are out, and we will be watching their portfolios closer than a hedge fund manager watches a short position [7]. We are also expanding this to include spousal and family ties, because the "my wife works for Pfizer, but I’m totally objective" excuse is officially retired [8].

### 3. The "Public Interest" Affidavit
Every panel member must sign a legally binding affidavit swearing that their primary loyalty is to the American public, not the quarterly earnings of a conglomerate [9]. Any individual found lying on this document will face federal perjury charges, a prospect that should be sufficiently terrifying to send the corporate shills running back to their boardrooms [10]. 

### 4. Why the Establishment is Terrified
The swamp creatures are clutching their pearls because we are threatening their gravy train, and they will inevitably label this move "anti-science" to protect their profit-extraction machines [11]. They do not want to help Robert F. Kennedy Jr. because he is the only one with the intestinal fortitude to turn the lights on in the basement and expose the rot [12]. We are going to make the industry so uncomfortable that they will be forced to actually produce healthy products just to remain relevant in the marketplace [13]. The swamp will not drain itself, Mr. President, but with these measures, we will ensure the NIH finally serves the people instead of the shareholders [14].
