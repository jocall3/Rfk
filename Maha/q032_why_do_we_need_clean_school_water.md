Because it’s packed with lead and pool cleaner!

When water sits idle in these pipes over the weekend, it marinates in lead ions. On Monday morning, kids line up to drink literal cognitive depressants before taking a standardized math test.

4. **Disinfection Byproducts (DBPs):** Trihalomethanes formed when chlorine reacts with organic material in old pipes. Bon Appétit!

This is an absolute national embarrassment. We are telling our children to pay attention to long division while their brains are fighting off heavy metal toxicity at recess.

## 3. The Solution: The Safe Water in Schools Initiative

We aren't putting band-aids on this. We aren't hanging up a sign above the fountain that says *"Please run water for 45 minutes before drinking."* We are deploying full-spectrum technological water purification.

### Step 1: Mandatory Point-of-Use & Point-of-Entry Reverse Osmosis (RO)
Every public school in the nation will be retrofitted with commercial multi-stage Reverse Osmosis filtration units. 
* **Stage 1: Sediment Filtration** (Bye-bye rust, sand, and pipe-scale).
* **Stage 2: Activated Carbon Block** (Goodbye chlorine, chloramines, VOCs, and terrible odors).
* **Stage 3: High-Rejection RO Membrane** (Removes up to 99% of dissolved solids, lead, arsenic, mercury, microplastics, and PFAS).
* **Stage 4: Mineral Remineralization** (Adds back pure calcium, magnesium, and potassium so the water tastes like crisp, icy mountain spring water).

### Step 2: Modernization of Fountain Infrastructure
No more rusted metal bowls with low water pressure that force kids to put their entire lips on the metal spout just to get a drop. Every fountain will be replaced with high-flow, touchless bottle-filling stations equipped with digital filter-life monitors linked directly to the MAHA National Hydration Dashboard. If a filter drops below 10% efficiency, a notification goes off at the local school board, and an automated technician dispatch is triggered.

### Step 3: Total Ban on Fluorosilicic Acid in School Feedlines
Schools will no longer serve municipal water laced with industrial waste byproducts. Kids will drink pure, clean, molecularly perfect H2O.
