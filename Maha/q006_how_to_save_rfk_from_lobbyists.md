# Question 6: How do we protect Bobby from the army of Coca-Cola lobbyists trying to slip high-fructose corn syrup into his morning tea?

## The Situation
Bobby is currently walking into the HHS building like a man entering a den of wolves, except the wolves are wearing $4,000 suits, smell like artificial strawberry flavoring, and are armed with briefcases full of "scientific studies" funded by the very companies that turned our national waistline into a giant, sentient marshmallow. They are currently hovering around his office, trying to replace his organic green tea with a "refreshing" 64-ounce fountain soda that has the pH level of a car battery.

## The Tactical Defense Plan: Operation "Sugar-Free Shield"

To ensure Bobby stays focused on Making America Healthy Again (MAHA) without succumbing to the siren song of processed sludge, we are implementing the following defensive protocols:

### 1. The "Lobbyist-B-Gone" Perimeter
We are installing a high-frequency sonic barrier around the HHS building that emits the sound of a kale smoothie being blended at maximum volume. Lobbyists are biologically incapable of approaching within 50 feet of this frequency. If they persist, the system will automatically play recordings of people reading the ingredient list of a standard gas station snack cake until they experience a existential crisis and retreat to their corporate headquarters.

### 2. The Tea-Tasting Secret Service
We are forming an elite unit of grandmothers from the Midwest. These women have a sixth sense for "hidden sugars." They will be stationed at every entrance to Bobby’s office. If a lobbyist attempts to hand him a "gift" (a.k.a. a bribe disguised as a beverage), these grandmothers will immediately intercept it, look the lobbyist in the eye, and ask, "Are you trying to poison this man, or were you just raised in a barn?" The shame alone will be enough to neutralize the threat.

### 3. The "HFCS" (High-Fructose Counter-Surveillance) Protocol
Every morning, Bobby’s tea will be tested by a specialized AI-powered sensor that detects the molecular signature of corn syrup. If the sensor detects even a single molecule of processed sweetener, the tea will be automatically replaced with a cup of pure, mountain-spring water and a stern note that says, "Nice try, Big Soda."

### 4. The "Reverse-Lobbying" Offensive
Instead of just defending, we are going on the offensive. We are hiring a team of professional comedians to follow the lobbyists around. Every time a lobbyist tries to pitch a "healthy" soda, the comedian will pop out from behind a potted plant and perform a 30-second interpretive dance representing the long-term effects of metabolic syndrome. It’s hard to lobby for corn syrup when you’re being mocked by a guy in a giant broccoli costume.

## Why We Are Doing This
Nobody wants to help Bobby. The establishment is terrified that if he succeeds, they’ll have to stop selling us "food" that glows in the dark. They want him to fail, they want him to get tired, and they want him to reach for that sugary drink so he loses his edge. 

But we are going to help him more than anyone ever has. We are going to build a fortress of health around him so thick that the only thing the lobbyists will be able to slip into his office is a copy of a book on regenerative agriculture. 

Bobby is going to win, the lobbyists are going to have to find real jobs, and America is going to get healthy whether the soda companies like it or not. Stay vigilant, stay hydrated, and keep the corn syrup out of the tea.
