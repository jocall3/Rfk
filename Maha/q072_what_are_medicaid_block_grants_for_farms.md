## The Solution: Medicaid Block Grants for Farms
We’re going to flip the script. Under Section 1115 demonstration authority, states have the power to get creative. We’re talking about taking up to 15% of Medicaid funds—money currently earmarked for "clinical" services—and redirecting it toward the *actual* root cause of health: nutrition.

We aren't just talking about "food stamps." We are talking about **Medicaid-funded Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) shares.**

### How it works:
1. **The 15% Pivot:** States apply for a waiver to treat "nutritional security" as a clinical intervention. If a doctor can prescribe a pill, they should be able to prescribe a box of organic, nutrient-dense vegetables grown three miles down the road.
2. **The Farm-to-Clinic Pipeline:** Instead of sending Medicaid dollars to a pharmaceutical conglomerate in a tax haven, we send them to Farmer Joe down the street. The state pays the farm, the farm delivers the food to the patient, and the patient gets healthy.
3. **The "Anti-Sickness" Strategy:** We make the people who profit from chronic illness absolutely furious. They’ll hate this because it actually works. When people start eating real food, they stop needing the "maintenance" drugs that keep the lobbyists’ yachts fueled.
