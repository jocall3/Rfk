### The Biochemistry of the Kid-Sized Foie Gras
The human liver is a masterpiece of biological engineering, but it was not designed to withstand the modern American dietary blitzkrieg. The core culprit behind pediatric NAFLD is the systemic inundation of **High-Fructose Corn Syrup (HFCS), refined carbohydrates, and industrial seed oils** hidden inside almost every product aimed at children.

1. **The Fructose Trap**: Unlike glucose, which every cell in the body can use for energy, **fructose can only be processed by the liver**. When a 10-year-old downs a single "fruit juice box" or a 20-ounce soda containing 65 grams of HFCS, the liver is flooded with concentrated liquid fructose.
2. **De Novo Lipogenesis (DNL)**: The liver takes this massive, unnatural fructose surge and rapidly converts it directly into fat via *De Novo Lipogenesis*. 
3. **Visceral Fat Accumulation**: Instead of burning this fuel, the liver stores it right inside its own cells. The result? Steatotic liver tissue—literally, a fatty liver—in kids who haven't even finished elementary school.
4. **Insulin Resistance & Inflammation**: As fat builds up in the hepatocytes (liver cells), local insulin resistance skyrockets, triggering systemic inflammation, elevated liver enzymes (ALT and AST), and early-stage fibrosis.
