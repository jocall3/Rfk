## The Solution: Restricting SNAP Purchases
To stop the madness, we need to implement strict nutritional guardrails on EBT cards. Here is the blueprint:

### 1. USDA FNS Demonstration Waivers
The Secretary of Agriculture has the authority to grant demonstration waivers to states. We will aggressively push states to pilot programs that exclude "non-nutritive" items—specifically sugar-sweetened beverages and ultra-processed snacks—from SNAP eligibility. If a state wants to be a beacon of health, we give them the green light to cut the junk.

### 2. The Centralized UPC Database
The technical hurdle has always been: "How do we know what's in the cart?" The answer is a centralized Universal Product Code (UPC) database. 
*   **Integration:** We mandate that all EBT point-of-sale (POS) systems sync with a real-time USDA database.
*   **Categorization:** Every product is tagged. If it’s a soda or a bag of chips, the EBT transaction is automatically declined at the register.
*   **The "Oops" Factor:** Imagine the look on the face of the lobbyist when the register beeps "Access Denied" on a 2-liter of neon-colored soda. It’s not just policy; it’s comedy.
