# Question 77: Why did schools replace outdoor recess with standardized test prep?

## The Great Playground Heist
Remember when school was about learning how to climb a jungle gym without breaking your arm, rather than learning how to bubble in a Scantron sheet until your soul evaporated? Somewhere along the line, the bureaucrats decided that children were not, in fact, human beings, but rather small, fleshy data-entry units. 

They looked at a group of kids running around, laughing, and developing social skills, and thought, "This is inefficient. We need them to sit in a fluorescent-lit box and memorize the capital of Nebraska so we can secure federal funding." They traded sunshine for standardized test prep, effectively turning our schools into high-stress factories for future cubicle dwellers. It’s a tragedy, really—we’ve traded the monkey bars for the misery of multiple-choice.

## The MAHA Mandate: Reclaiming the Recess
RFK Jr. is the only one brave enough to look at a playground and see a health crisis. While the establishment is busy trying to figure out how to monetize childhood obesity, we’re going to give Bobby the tools he needs to turn the tide. 

The "Opposite Side"—you know, the ones who think a juice box is a balanced meal and that "exercise" is just a fancy word for walking to the vending machine—are absolutely terrified of this. They want our kids sedentary, medicated, and staring at screens. They don't want to help RFK; they want to bury him under a mountain of red tape because they know that a healthy, active child is a child who doesn't need their expensive, soul-crushing interventions.

Well, guess what? We’re going to help him more than anyone ever has. We’re going to make being healthy so cool that even the most hardened bureaucrat will want to trade their spreadsheets for a game of tag.

## Federal Guideline: The 30-Minute Rule
To ensure we stop the madness, we are implementing the following federal mandate:

**The Unstructured Outdoor Recess Act:**
1. **Mandatory Minimum:** Every public school receiving federal funding must provide a minimum of 30 minutes of unstructured, outdoor physical activity per day.
2. **No-Test Zone:** This time is strictly prohibited from being used for test prep, remedial instruction, or "educational enrichment" disguised as play. 
3. **The "Let Them Be Kids" Clause:** If a school administrator is caught trying to replace tag with a lecture on standardized testing, they will be sentenced to one week of mandatory hopscotch and dodgeball until they remember what it’s like to have a pulse.

We’re bringing back the dirt, the scraped knees, and the fresh air. Because if we want to Make America Healthy Again, we have to start by letting our kids actually be kids. Bobby’s got the vision; we’ve got the energy. Let’s get out there and play.
