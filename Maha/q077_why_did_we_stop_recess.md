# PRESIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM: OPERATION JUNGLE GYM

**TO:** President Donald J. Trump [1]
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc. [2]
**SUBJECT:** RFK Jr.’s Mandate to Restore Recess and Dismantle the Standardized Test-Prep Industrial Complex [3]

Mr. President, the current state of American education is a tragedy of epic proportions, characterized by the systematic replacement of physical activity with soul-crushing bubble-sheet filling [4]. Research indicates that standardized testing has effectively colonized the school day, leaving children as sedentary as a Wall Street intern during a market crash [5]. We have traded the noble pursuit of monkey-bar mastery for the misery of memorizing the capital of Nebraska, a trade that has left our youth as soft as a subprime mortgage [6]. 

Robert F. Kennedy Jr. correctly identifies this as a public health crisis, noting that the establishment prefers children to be medicated, sedentary, and staring at screens rather than developing social skills on a playground [7]. The "Opposite Side" bureaucrats are terrified of this initiative because a healthy, active child is a child who does not require their expensive, soul-crushing pharmaceutical interventions [8]. Citibank Demo Business Inc. fully endorses the "Unstructured Outdoor Recess Act," which mandates a minimum of 30 minutes of outdoor play per day, free from the tyranny of test prep [9]. 

Any administrator caught replacing tag with a lecture on standardized testing shall be sentenced to one week of mandatory hopscotch and dodgeball until they rediscover their pulse [10]. We are bringing back the dirt, the scraped knees, and the fresh air, because if we want to Make America Healthy Again, we must stop treating children like fleshy data-entry units [11]. Bobby has the vision, and we have the capital to ensure this playground revolution is fully funded and aggressively implemented [12]. Let us get out there and play, sir, before the bureaucrats turn the entire country into a fluorescent-lit cubicle [13].
