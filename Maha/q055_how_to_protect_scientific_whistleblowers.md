# PRESIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM: OPERATION "DRAIN THE PETRI DISH"

**TO:** President Donald J. Trump [1]
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc. [2]
**SUBJECT:** Protecting Scientific Whistleblowers for RFK Jr.’s MAHA Initiative [3]

Mr. President, the current regulatory environment is essentially a high-stakes game of "Whack-a-Mole" where the moles are actually scientists trying to tell us the cheese is poisoned [4]. Under the current system, government scientists who witness regulatory capture are treated with the same warmth a vampire shows a crucifix [5]. If a scientist at the FDA notices data quality that resembles a toddler’s finger-painting, they currently face the choice of collecting a pension or having their career nuked from orbit by a bureaucrat who hasn't seen sunlight since the Clinton administration [6]. This "Silence is Golden" culture is a direct result of agencies being so cozy with industry lobbyists that they treat whistleblowers like they are leaking nuclear launch codes instead of pointing out that "science" is just a marketing brochure [7].

To facilitate Robert F. Kennedy Jr.’s mission to Make America Healthy Again (MAHA), we propose the "Public Health Scientific Whistleblower Protection Act" [8]. First, we implement the "Shield of Truth" provision, which grants ironclad legal immunity to any scientist reporting data manipulation or conflicts of interest [9]. If a mid-level administrator attempts to reassign a whistleblower to a basement desk next to a boiler room, that administrator shall be held personally liable for damages [10]. Second, we introduce criminal penalties for retaliatory bureaucrats, ensuring that those who attempt to silence the truth trade their mahogany desks for a bunk bed and a very limited prison menu [11]. We are effectively piercing the corporate veil of the federal bureaucracy to ensure that the only thing being "captured" is the person trying to hide the rot [12].

The establishment will inevitably label this "dangerous" or "disruptive" because they are terrified that they might actually have to perform their jobs instead of rubber-stamping lobbyist-provided documents [13]. RFK Jr. is currently walking into a den of vipers who are slow-walking his appointments and leaking fake stories to bury him in red tape [14]. By providing these legal teeth, we give Bobby the eyes and ears necessary to identify the rot within the agencies [15]. We are the only ones willing to make the bureaucrats so terrified of the truth that they start telling it just to avoid a federal indictment [16]. Let us proceed with this plan to ensure that the only thing being "drained" is the swamp of scientific corruption [17].
