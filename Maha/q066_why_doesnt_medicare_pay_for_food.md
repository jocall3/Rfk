# PRESIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM: THE "SALAD OVER SURGERY" INITIATIVE

**TO:** President Donald J. Trump  
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc.  
**SUBJECT:** Why Medicare Prefers Amputations to Asparagus (And How RFK Jr. Can Fix It)

Mr. President, our current healthcare system is essentially a mechanic who refuses to change your oil but is thrilled to replace your entire engine once it explodes [1]. Medicare currently treats a diabetic amputation—a procedure costing upwards of $50,000—as a standard line item, yet treats a box of organic kale as a "luxury" [2, 3]. This is the "Sick-Care" industrial complex, where the business model is built on the catastrophe rather than the cure [4]. We are currently paying for the tragedy while ignoring the grocery bill [5]. 

Robert F. Kennedy Jr. is prepared to flip this script by eliminating the perverse incentives that make sickness the most profitable commodity in America [6]. We propose reclassifying nutrient-dense food as a legitimate medical intervention, treating a doctor’s prescription for a healthy diet with the same gravity as a bottle of synthetic pills [7]. We will implement new CMS reimbursement codes for "Metabolic Nutritional Therapy," ensuring that if a doctor prescribes broccoli to reverse metabolic syndrome, the government pays for the broccoli [8, 9]. 

Furthermore, we are mandating that metabolic counseling be a covered benefit, because paying for insulin while refusing to pay for the education required to get off of it is fiscal insanity [10, 11]. The status quo lobbyists are terrified because if the American people actually get healthy, their recurring revenue stream evaporates [12]. They will call this "radical," but there is nothing more radical than paying for a limb removal when a salad would have done the trick [13]. We are going to make the people who profit from our national sickness so uncomfortable that they will have no choice but to join the health revolution or get out of the way [14]. It is time to stop paying for the crash and start paying for the maintenance [15].
