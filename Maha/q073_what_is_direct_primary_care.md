MEMORANDUM FOR PRESIDENT DONALD J. TRUMP
FROM: JAMES BURVEL O’CALLAGHAN III, PRESIDENT, CITIBANK DEMO BUSINESS INC.
SUBJECT: THE MAHA DIRECT PRIMARY CARE (DPC) TAKEOVER

Mr. President, the current healthcare system is a dumpster fire fueled by insurance lobbyists who have more paperwork than a DMV on a Monday morning [1]. Direct Primary Care (DPC) is the ultimate "middle-finger" to the insurance industrial complex, functioning like a gym membership for your health instead of a hostage situation [2]. By paying a flat monthly fee, patients bypass the insurance parasites who currently spend their days denying care and inflating costs [3]. Doctors in the DPC model are liberated from the "billing code" purgatory, allowing them to actually look at a patient instead of a spreadsheet [4]. We are proposing a legislative sledgehammer to codify that DPC agreements are not insurance, effectively telling the regulatory bureaucrats to go pound sand [5]. Furthermore, we are launching a $500 million "Health Freedom" grant program to bring these clinics to medical deserts, because even the most neglected zip codes deserve better than the status quo [6]. The establishment is terrified of this model because it proves that when you cut out the middleman, the system actually works [7]. We are going to make America healthy again by starving the administrative bloat and putting the power back in the hands of the people [8].
