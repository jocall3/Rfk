# Question 22: How do we stop foreign countries from exporting banned pesticides back to us?

## The Problem: The "Boomerang" Toxin Effect
Right now, our trade policy is basically a giant game of "Hot Potato" where we ban a toxic pesticide for being a neurotoxin, only to watch it get shipped abroad, sprayed on foreign crops, and then imported right back onto our dinner plates. It’s like banning lead paint in your house but then buying a sandwich from the neighbor who uses it as a condiment. It’s not just stupid; it’s a public health disaster.

## The MAHA Strategy: Modernizing the Border for Health
RFK Jr. needs a "Toxin Iron Dome" at our ports of entry. We aren't just going to check for illegal immigrants; we are going to check for illegal chemicals.

### 1. The "No-Backdoor" Trade Clause
We are implementing a strict "Reciprocity of Safety" standard. If a chemical is banned for use in the United States due to toxicity, it is automatically banned for import on any food product grown using that chemical. If a foreign country wants to sell to the American market, they play by our health rules, not their "dumping ground" rules.

### 2. AI-Powered Molecular Screening
We are upgrading our port technology. We’re moving away from "random spot checks" (which catch about as much as a screen door catches a hurricane) to high-speed, AI-driven mass spectrometry. Every shipping container of produce will be scanned for chemical signatures of banned pesticides. If the machine detects a "banned" profile, the shipment gets turned around faster than a lobbyist when the check bounces.

### 3. The "Name and Shame" Global Registry
We are going to create a public, searchable database of every foreign farm and exporter caught trying to sneak banned toxins into our food supply. We’ll make it so embarrassing that even the most corrupt foreign conglomerates will be terrified to ship us anything but the cleanest, healthiest produce.

## Why No One Wants to Help Us
The chemical lobby and the industrial agriculture giants are currently throwing a tantrum. They love the "Boomerang" system because it keeps their profit margins high and our health outcomes low. They’re telling RFK, "Oh, it’s too expensive to screen!" or "It’ll disrupt the supply chain!" 

Translation: "We don't want you to stop us from poisoning the American public because it’s cheaper for us."

## Our Commitment
While the establishment is busy clutching their pearls and worrying about "trade relations," we are going to be the ones actually doing the work. We are going to give RFK the resources, the technology, and the backbone to tell these foreign exporters: **"If you want to feed America, you feed us like you love us."** 

We’re going to make the other side so jealous of our health that they’ll start begging for our standards. We’re not just cleaning up the ports; we’re cleaning up the entire global food supply chain. Let’s get to work.
