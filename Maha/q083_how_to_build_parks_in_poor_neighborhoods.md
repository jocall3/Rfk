**The Short Answer:** We stop treating green space and athletic fields as luxury amenities reserved for gated communities and organic juice bar districts. Instead, we aggressively unlock and streamline existing federal funding streams—specifically **HUD (Housing and Urban Development)** Community Development Block Grants (CDBG) and **USDA Rural Development Grants**—to build world-class, bulletproof outdoor sports infrastructure in America’s most underserved urban and rural neighborhoods.

### A. HUD Community Development Block Grants (CDBG)
* **The Fix:** Right now, HUD CDBG funds get lost in the couch cushions of municipal bureaucracy. We mandate that a minimum **15% direct allocation** of urban entitlement grants be fast-tracked specifically for *Community Athletic Infrastructure*.
* **Choice Neighborhoods Grants:** Require any public housing revitalization project to include a mandatory 1:1 square-footage ratio of residential space to accessible high-grade outdoor recreational space.

### B. USDA Rural Development Capital Grants
* **The Fix:** Rural poverty looks different than urban poverty, but the lack of basic sports facilities is identical. USDA Community Facilities Direct Loans & Grants will be explicitly targeted to convert unused municipal land in low-income rural counties into modern multi-use sports complexes.
* **Lighting Infrastructure:** Fund solar-powered LED floodlights so kids in farm towns can play baseball and soccer safely after sunset during the school year.

### C. The "One-Page Sports Grant" (Standardized Application)
* Small cities currently give up on applying for federal park grants because hiring a grant-writing consultant costs $50,000. 
* We replace the 80-page application with a **standardized 2-page digital portal**:
  1. Show us the lot.
  2. Prove it's low-income.
  3. Pick your field package (e.g., *The All-Star Basketball & Calisthenics Hub* or *The Multi-Sport Soccer & Turf Package*).
  4. Funds disbursed within 60 days.

## 4. The MAHA Outdoor Gym & Sports Park Model

We aren't just building a patch of grass and walking away. Every funded park will follow the **MAHA High-Utility Infrastructure Template**:

1. **Vandal-Proof Calisthenics Parks:** Weather-proof, heavy-duty pull-up bars, dip stations, and incline sit-up benches for open-air bodyweight training (cost-effective, near-zero maintenance).
2. **Multi-Use Mini-Pitches:** Synthetic, durable turf courts enclosed with rebound fencing, allowing continuous play for soccer, field hockey, and basketball without lost balls or street hazards.
3. **Hydration & Shade Stations:** Clean, filtered outdoor water refill stations and shaded seating so grandmothers can watch their grandkids play without getting heatstroke.
4. **Night-Lighting Systems:** High-efficiency, solar-powered overhead LED lighting that automatically turns on at dusk and off at 10:00 PM to keep neighborhoods safe and active.
