# PRESIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM: THE "HEALTH-OR-BUST" BUREAUCRAT BONANZA

**TO:** President Donald J. Trump  
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc.  
**SUBJECT:** Tying Bureaucrat Bonuses to Actual Human Vitality (The "Make Them Sweat" Initiative)

Mr. President, the current health bureaucracy is essentially a group of arsonists who get paid bonuses for how many fire extinguishers they sell while the house burns down [1]. We propose a radical shift: if the American people aren't getting healthier, the bureaucrats shouldn't be getting richer [2]. Under the current Senior Executive Service (SES) structure, federal employees receive performance awards regardless of whether the national health trajectory is a dumpster fire [3]. We are proposing that Robert F. Kennedy Jr. implement a "Health-or-Bust" KPI system where bonuses are strictly tethered to the reduction of chronic disease markers [4]. 

### THE STRATEGY FOR RFK JR.

1. **THE METABOLIC METRIC:** Bureaucrats will no longer be compensated for "process" or "number of meetings attended," as these are merely bureaucratic theater designed to mask systemic failure [5]. Instead, bonuses will be triggered exclusively by a measurable, statistically significant decline in national Type 2 diabetes and childhood obesity rates [6]. 
2. **THE "SWAMP-DRAIN" CLAUSE:** Any bureaucrat who claims they cannot influence public health outcomes will be immediately identified as "deadwood" and replaced by a patriot who understands that their paycheck is a reflection of the public's vitality [7]. 
3. **FINANCIAL ALIGNMENT:** By tying the wallets of the administrative state to the wellness of the citizenry, we effectively turn the pharmaceutical lobby’s greatest assets into the public’s most aggressive health advocates [8]. 
4. **ELIMINATING THE "SICK-CARE" PROFIT MOTIVE:** Currently, the system treats the American population as a "recurring revenue stream" rather than a constituency to be served [9]. We will flip this model so that the only way for a bureaucrat to afford their third vacation home is to ensure the American people are actually thriving [10]. 

Mr. President, this plan will make the swamp creatures scream louder than a lobbyist who just lost their expense account, which is exactly how we know it’s working [11]. We are ready to provide the data, the legislative teeth, and the sheer audacity required to make this happen [12].
