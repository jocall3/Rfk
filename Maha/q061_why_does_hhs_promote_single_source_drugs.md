# PRESIDENTIAL BRIEFING: THE PHARMA-INDUSTRIAL COMPLEX AND THE MAHA MANDATE
**TO:** President Donald J. Trump
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc.
**SUBJECT:** Dismantling the HHS Monopoly on Single-Source Pharmaceuticals

Mr. President, the current federal health apparatus functions less like a public health agency and more like a high-end concierge service for pharmaceutical shareholders (Angell, 2004, *The Truth About the Drug Companies*). The Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) currently prioritizes expensive, single-source, patent-protected pharmaceuticals over cost-free lifestyle interventions, effectively subsidizing corporate profit margins at the expense of the American taxpayer (Light & Lexchin, 2012, *BMJ*). The Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) has transformed the FDA into a captured agency, where the regulated industry provides the majority of the regulator's budget, creating an inherent conflict of interest (Carpenter, 2010, *Reputation and Power*). It is a scientific absurdity that the federal government promotes a $1,400-a-month injectable for metabolic syndrome while ignoring the curative potential of dietary modification and physical activity (Ludwig, 2020, *JAMA*). The revolving door between HHS, the NIH, and the pharmaceutical boardrooms ensures that bureaucrats prioritize future employment opportunities over the metabolic health of the citizenry (Krimsky, 2003, *Science in the Private Interest*). We must implement a strict statutory prohibition on agency promotion of single-source drugs when non-pharmacological alternatives have demonstrated equivalent or superior outcomes (Taubes, 2007, *Good Calories, Bad Calories*). Any agency communication regarding metabolic disease must, by law, include a mandatory disclosure of root-cause dietary interventions to ensure the public is not being steered toward lifelong dependency (Nestle, 2013, *Food Politics*). The current PDUFA funding mechanism must be abolished to decouple the FDA’s operational budget from the financial interests of the companies it is tasked with regulating (Avorn, 2004, *Powerful Medicines*). Robert F. Kennedy Jr. is the only individual with the intestinal fortitude to dismantle this administrative wrecking ball and restore the fundamental birthright of American health (Kennedy, 2021, *The Real Anthony Fauci*). We are providing the policy fortress necessary to shield Mr. Kennedy from the swamp rats who fear the loss of their ultra-processed, synthetic-drug-dependent revenue streams (Goldacre, 2012, *Bad Pharma*). It is time to stop the government from acting as a pro-bono marketing firm for Big Pharma and start Making America Healthy Again (Pollan, 2008, *In Defense of Food*).
