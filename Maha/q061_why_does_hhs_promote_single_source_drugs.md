# MAHA Policy Blueprint | Question 61: Why Does HHS Promote Expensive Single-Source Pharmaceuticals Instead of Cheap Lifestyle Interventions?

**Question 61:** Why does the federal health bureaucracy—specifically the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), FDA, and NIH—spend billions promoting outrageously expensive, patented, single-source drugs while completely ignoring dirt-cheap (or free!) lifestyle changes, metabolic interventions, and real food? What statutory and regulatory restrictions must be placed on agency promotion of monopoly pharmaceuticals?

---

## 1. The Absurd Reality: $1,400-a-Month Jab vs. A Morning Walk and a Grass-Fed Steak

Let’s state the obvious: if walking 30 minutes a day, lifting heavy objects, getting sunlight, and cutting out toxic industrial seed oils were patented by Pfizer, every cable news host in America would be wearing a wearable patch delivering automated prompts to "SQUAT NOW OR DIE."

Instead, our health agencies act as the premier pro-bono marketing firm for single-source (patent-protected) pharmaceuticals. When a mega-corporation invents a proprietary synthetic molecule that suppresses a symptom at the low, low price of $1,400 per month for life, HHS practically throws a ticker-tape parade. Meanwhile, if a doctor tells a patient, *"Hey, maybe stop drinking three liters of high-fructose corn syrup a day and your fatty liver will clear up,"* the establishment acts as if that doctor just performed unauthorized voodoo.

Why? Because **there is no multi-billion-dollar lobby for broccoli, squats, and sleep.** You can’t patent a pull-up. You can’t put a 20-year monopoly barrier on circadian rhythm alignment. 

---

## 2. The Mechanics of the Monopoly Trap: Follow the User Fees and Revolving Doors

Why does HHS default to promoting single-source brand-name drugs?

1. **PDUFA & Payer Capture:** Over half of the FDA’s Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) budget comes from **Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA)** dollars paid directly by the pharmaceutical giants. The agency is literally funded by the companies it is supposed to regulate. When was the last time the "National Sunshine & Exercise Board" paid PDUFA fees to FDA? Never.
2. **The Revolving Door Extravaganza:** High-ranking bureaucrats finish a stint at HHS, FDA, or NIH and magically land $2 million-a-year board seats at companies producing single-source drugs. Pushing a patent drug guarantees a cozy retirement; pushing lifestyle medicine gets you labeled an "unscientific folk healer" at the agency watercooler.
3. **The "Gold Standard" Fallacy:** HHS hides behind "randomized controlled trials" designed specifically *for* single-molecule chemical patents, while claiming complex lifestyle interventions are "too hard to study." Translation: *We refused to study it because no drug manufacturer funded the trial.*

## 3. Statutory Restrictions Required: Banning Agency Promotion of Single-Source Pharmaceuticals

To clean up this swamp and give **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.** the exact policy artillery he needs to smash this racket, we must institute strict statutory prohibitions on agency cheerleading for single-source drugs:

### A. Strict Prohibition on Agency Endorsements & Unsolicited Guidance
HHS, FDA, CDC, and NIH personnel shall be legally barred from issuing public health guidance, press releases, or clinical toolkits that endorse or prioritize a single-source, patent-protected pharmaceutical when non-pharmacological, dietary, or off-patent generic alternatives have demonstrated equivalent or superior long-term metabolic outcomes.

### B. Mandatory Equal-Time Lifestyle Impact Disclosures
Any agency communication discussing a pharmaceutical intervention for metabolic diseases (e.g., Type 2 Diabetes, Obesity, Hypertension, Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease) must allocate **equal prominent space and airtime** detailing:
* Root-cause dietary causes of the pathology.
* Zero-cost lifestyle interventions proven to reverse the condition.
* The long-term costs and dependency risks associated with lifelong single-source drug use.

### C. Elimination of Corporate PDUFA Capture
Eliminate PDUFA funding mechanisms that allow drug companies to fast-track single-source blockbusters while agency staff ignore basic preventative health sciences.
