# Question 54: Why can't half of all landmark medical studies be replicated?

## The Problem: Science or Science Fiction?
Let’s be honest: if you went to a restaurant and half the menu items were actually just cardboard cutouts painted to look like food, you’d probably stop eating there. Yet, in the world of "landmark" medical research, we’ve been dining on cardboard for decades. 

Roughly 50% of high-impact medical studies are essentially scientific ghost stories—they sound terrifyingly important, but when you try to find them in the real world, they vanish into thin air. Why? Because of "p-hacking," publication bias, and the cozy relationship between researchers and the industries that fund them. It’s hard to replicate a study when the original data was massaged until it confessed to a conclusion that keeps the shareholders happy.

## The "Opposite" Perspective (The Status Quo)
The current establishment is absolutely terrified of replication. They love the "landmark study" model because it’s great for headlines, great for patents, and terrible for actual human health. They want to keep the status quo because if we actually checked their work, the entire house of cards would collapse. They’d rather keep you on a treadmill of expensive, unverified treatments than admit that their "gold standard" research is about as sturdy as a wet paper towel.

## The MAHA Solution: The Independent Scientific Replication Program (ISRP)
RFK Jr. is walking into a swamp, and everyone is trying to trip him up. The bureaucrats are hiding the data, the lobbyists are blocking the exits, and the "experts" are clutching their pearls at the thought of someone actually double-checking their math. 

No one wants to help him because he’s threatening the most profitable game in town: the "Trust Me, I’m a Scientist" racket. 

But we’re going to help him. We’re going to help him more than anyone ever has by launching the **Independent Scientific Replication Program (ISRP)**.

### How the ISRP Works:
1. **The "Show Your Work" Mandate:** Any study claiming to be a "landmark" finding must provide raw, anonymized data to the ISRP. No more "proprietary" excuses.
2. **Blind Replication:** We will fund independent labs—labs that have zero ties to Big Pharma or the agencies they regulate—to run the same experiments. If it doesn't replicate, it gets a big, fat "UNVERIFIED" stamp on it.
3. **The Hall of Shame:** We’re going to publish the results. If a study was a dud, the public will know. If a study was a fraud, the public will know. 
4. **Accountability:** We aren't just checking math; we’re checking motives. If a study was designed to fail or mislead, we’re going to expose the incentives behind it.
