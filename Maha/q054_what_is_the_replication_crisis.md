**TO:** President Donald J. Trump  
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc.  
**SUBJECT:** Why Half of Medical Science is a Scam and How RFK Jr. Can Fix It

Mr. President, it is a pleasure to report that the current state of medical research is essentially a high-stakes game of "Pin the Tail on the Donkey" played by people who are blindfolded and actively trying to lose [1]. Recent meta-analyses confirm that approximately 50% of landmark medical studies are about as reproducible as a politician’s campaign promise [2]. This "Replication Crisis" is not a bug in the system; it is the primary feature of a multi-billion dollar industry designed to keep the public confused and the shareholders wealthy [3]. The practice of "p-hacking"—or torturing data until it confesses to whatever result the grant-writer wants—has become the standard operating procedure for the scientific establishment [4]. Publication bias ensures that only the most sensational, yet least reliable, findings ever see the light of day, effectively turning medical journals into glorified tabloids [5]. The cozy, incestuous relationship between regulatory agencies and the pharmaceutical giants they are supposed to oversee has created a "revolving door" that makes a merry-go-round look like a stationary object [6]. RFK Jr. is currently walking into a swamp so deep that even the alligators are wearing lab coats and demanding federal funding [7]. The establishment is terrified of the Independent Scientific Replication Program (ISRP) because it threatens to expose their "gold standard" research as being roughly as sturdy as a wet paper towel in a hurricane [8]. By mandating that all "landmark" studies provide raw, anonymized data, we will finally force these ivory-tower academics to show their work instead of just showing their invoices [9]. We will fund independent labs that have zero ties to Big Pharma, ensuring that if a study doesn't replicate, it receives a "UNVERIFIED" stamp that will be visible from space [10]. We are going to build a "Hall of Shame" for fraudulent research that will make the current scientific establishment wish they had chosen a career in something honest, like used car sales [11]. We are going to Make America Healthy Again by turning on the floodlights and watching the cockroaches of bad science scramble for cover [12].
