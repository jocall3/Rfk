# Question 69: Why are metabolic screenings not free?

## The Problem: The "Sick-Care" Tollbooth
Right now, if you want to check your fasting insulin—the single most important metric for understanding if you’re sliding toward metabolic disaster—insurance companies treat it like you’re trying to buy a luxury yacht. They slap a copay on it, make you jump through hoops, and act like you’re being "frivolous" for wanting to know if your body is actually processing energy or just turning into a sugar-storage facility.

It’s almost as if the current system *wants* you to stay in the dark until you’re already deep into Type 2 diabetes territory. Why? Because a healthy person is a terrible customer for the pharmaceutical industrial complex. They don't want you catching the fire; they want to sell you the extinguisher after your house has already burned down.

## The MAHA Mandate: Free Metabolic Intelligence
Under the MAHA (Make America Healthy Again) framework, we are flipping the script. We are mandating that advanced metabolic health screenings—including fasting insulin, HbA1c, and lipid panels—be classified as **zero-copay preventative services** under the Affordable Care Act (ACA).

### The Plan:
1.  **Mandatory Coverage:** We are updating the ACA preventative care list to include comprehensive metabolic panels. If you want to know your numbers, the insurance company pays. Period.
2.  **Removing the "Sick-Care" Barrier:** No more "medical necessity" gatekeeping. If you are a human being living in America, you have a right to know your metabolic status without a financial penalty.
3.  **Data for the People:** By making these tests free, we create a massive, anonymized dataset that helps us track the health of the nation in real-time, rather than waiting for the next chronic disease epidemic to hit the headlines.
