**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc.  
**SUBJECT:** Neutralizing the NIH Prestige Cartel for RFK Jr.’s MAHA Initiative

Mr. President, the NIH grant process is currently a glorified country club where the only thing being cured is the boredom of tenured professors at Ivy League institutions [1]. We have analyzed the data, and it is clear that the "Prestige Trap" is a systemic failure that prioritizes pedigree over actual scientific utility [2]. To Make America Healthy Again (MAHA), we must implement a double-blind review process that treats institutional pedigree like a bad case of the flu [3]. 

1. **Identity Scrubbing:** We will strip all investigator names and institutional affiliations from grant applications, forcing reviewers to actually read the science instead of checking if the applicant has a yacht in the Hamptons [4]. 
2. **The "Idea-First" Protocol:** By presenting the core hypothesis before the CV, we ensure that brilliant outsiders aren't discarded simply because they didn't attend the same cocktail parties as the review board [5]. 
3. **The "Moonshot" Contrarian Track:** We are establishing a dedicated lane for high-risk, high-reward research, reviewed by a panel of outsiders who aren't currently feeding at the federal trough [6]. 

The current gatekeepers are terrified of this transition because their entire business model relies on the "Rolodex Effect" rather than empirical merit [7]. They will inevitably claim this is "unscientific," which is bureaucratic code for "please don't stop funding my 15th redundant study on the same tired topic" [8]. RFK Jr. is prepared to drain this swamp, and we are providing the tactical transparency required to make opposition politically radioactive [9]. We will publish these blinded proposals in a public database, allowing the American people to see exactly which geniuses the status quo has been suppressing [10]. It is time to stop funding the past and start funding the future, Mr. President; let’s make the establishment so uncomfortable they’ll start exercising just to keep up [11].
