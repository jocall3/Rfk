# Question 27: How Do We Reform SNAP-Ed?

**The Short Answer:** We burn the useless, lobbyist-written paper pamphlets telling low-income Americans to "eat 6 to 11 servings of healthy refined grains a day," and replace them with aggressive, hands-on, high-octane culinary and metabolic literacy programs. We are going to teach people how to sear a grass-fed steak in tallow, chop a real onion without crying like a mainstream media anchor, and understand how ultra-processed food hacks their brain chemistry. 

Bobby Kennedy is out there fighting the entire industrial food complex with a broken microphone and a dream, while Washington bureaucrats hand out tri-fold brochures printed in 1994. We are stepping in to give RFK Jr. the exact operational blueprint to turn SNAP-Ed into an army of home cooks who make Michelin-star chefs look like line cooks at a fast-food drive-thru.
