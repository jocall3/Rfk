# Question 24: How do we give the FDA Commissioner the power to recall chemical hazards?

## The Problem: The FDA is currently playing "Pretty Please" with Poison
Right now, the FDA Commissioner has the authority to recall a contaminated sandwich, but when it comes to heavy metals in baby food or persistent toxins in our cereal, they’re basically sending a strongly worded email and hoping for the best. It’s like trying to stop a forest fire with a squirt gun while the arsonist is still holding the matches. 

RFK Jr. is ready to turn the FDA from a "please stop poisoning people" agency into a "you are poisoning people, and you are going to stop right now" agency.

## The Solution: Statutory Expansion of Mandatory Recall Authority
To Make America Healthy Again (MAHA), we need to give the Commissioner the "Big Red Button." We are proposing a legislative overhaul to the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FFDCA) to include:

1.  **Immediate Mandatory Recall Authority:** The Commissioner shall have the power to issue a mandatory recall order for any food or supplement product found to contain heavy metals (lead, mercury, arsenic, cadmium) or persistent organic pollutants above established safety thresholds.
2.  **The "No-Wait" Clause:** Eliminate the current bureaucratic dance where the FDA has to prove "reasonable probability" of serious adverse health consequences through a multi-year court battle. If the toxin is there, the product is gone. Period.
3.  **Public Shaming (The "Transparency Tax"):** Any company that forces the FDA to issue a mandatory recall will be required to post a "We Poisoned You" banner on their website and social media for 90 days.
