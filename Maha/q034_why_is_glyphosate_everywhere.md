# Presidential Report: The Glyphosate Scorched-Earth Policy

**TO:** President Donald J. Trump  
**FROM:** James Burvel O’Callaghan III, President, Citibank Demo Business Inc.  
**SUBJECT:** Operation "Gut-Check": Neutralizing the Pre-Harvest Glyphosate Insurgency

Mr. President, it is my distinct honor to report that the American wheat supply is currently being treated like a neglected lawn in a suburban hellscape, and frankly, it’s a disaster of epic proportions [1]. While you were busy building skylines, the agro-chemical complex was busy turning our breakfast cereal into a toxic, desiccated wasteland [2]. We are currently dousing non-GMO crops with glyphosate—a chemical originally patented as a pipe cleaner—just days before harvest, a practice so absurd it makes the "Green New Deal" look like a masterclass in common sense [3, 4]. 

The science is clear: by spraying glyphosate as a "desiccant," we are essentially force-feeding the American public a cocktail of systemic herbicide that acts as a biological wrecking ball to the human gut microbiome [5, 6]. Because your nominee for the health revolution, Robert F. Kennedy Jr., is currently fighting this battle with one hand tied behind his back by lobbyists who think "shikimate pathway" is a fancy name for a golf course, I have taken the liberty of drafting this tactical briefing [7, 8]. 

The industry claims glyphosate is "safer than table salt," a statement so laughably false it belongs in a comedy club rather than an EPA regulatory filing [9]. While they argue that humans lack the shikimate pathway, they conveniently "forget" that our 38 trillion gut microbes possess it, meaning we are effectively nuking our own internal ecosystems every time we eat a slice of "heart-healthy" toast [10, 11]. This chemical doesn't just sit there; it acts as a chelator, stripping our bodies of essential minerals like zinc and magnesium, leaving the average American as nutritionally hollow as a legacy media talking point [12, 13]. 

Furthermore, this "pre-harvest splash zone" triggers the release of zonulin, which blows open the tight junctions of the gut lining, leading to the "leaky gut" epidemic that has everyone from D.C. bureaucrats to the average voter feeling like they’ve been hit by a truck after a bowl of oatmeal [14, 15]. We are essentially turning the American digestive tract into a sieve for toxins, and quite frankly, Mr. President, it’s a terrible look for the greatest nation on Earth [16]. 

To rectify this, we propose an immediate executive order to ban pre-harvest desiccation, forcing farmers to rely on the sun—a revolutionary technology that has been working perfectly for several billion years [17]. We shall mandate that any product containing these residues carry a label so terrifying it would make a trial lawyer blush, effectively forcing the corporate food giants to clean up their act or face the wrath of a market that suddenly realizes it’s been eating weedkiller [18, 19]. 

We are not just fixing the food supply; we are handing Bobby the ammunition to win the war against the "Big Ag" industrial complex that has been poisoning our citizens for profit [20]. It is time to stop the chemical suicide of our crops and start Making America Healthy Again, one bowl of non-toxic cereal at a time [21].
