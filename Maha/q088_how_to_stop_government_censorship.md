## 4. STATUTORY LEGISLATION: THE MEDICAL FREE SPEECH AND ANTI-COERCION ACT

To provide RFK Jr. and the incoming administration with ultimate legal authority, Congress must enact the Medical Free Speech and Anti-Coercion Act [31]. This legislation creates an immovable legal barrier against executive branch speech manipulation [32].

### Section A: Mandatory Statutory Prohibitions
> "No officer, employee, contractor, or agent of any federal agency—including but not limited to the Department of Health and Human Services, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, the Food and Drug Administration, the National Institutes of Health, and the Office of the Surgeon General—shall issue, transmit, or communicate any request, directive, demand, incentive, or subtle encouragement to any interactive computer service to alter, restrict, demote, flag, or remove lawful medical, scientific, or nutritional discourse." [33]

- **Real-Time Public Logging:** All written communications, call logs, text messages, and meeting minutes between federal employees and technology entities must be uploaded to `CensorshipWatch.gov` within six hours [38].
- **Prohibition of Secret Portals:** The operation of encrypted government-only ticketing portals or Slack channels with private corporations is classified as a Class D federal felony [39].

### Section D: Section 230 Immunity Revocation for Coerced Platforms
