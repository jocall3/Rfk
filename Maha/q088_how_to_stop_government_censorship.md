## 3. Statutory Prohibitions: The Legal Sledgehammer

To give RFK Jr. the legal firepower he needs, Congress must pass (or the Executive Branch must strictly enforce via constitutional precedent) the **Medical Free Speech and Anti-Coercion Act**. Here is the blueprint:

### A. The Federal Jawboning Prohibition Act (Statutory Drafting)
**Provision 1: Absolute Prohibition on Executive Speech Suppressions**
> "No officer, employee, or agent of any federal agency—including but not limited to HHS, CDC, FDA, NIH, and the office of the Surgeon General—shall request, pressure, incentivize, or mandate any private interactive computer service (as defined in 47 U.S.C. § 230) to remove, suppress, shadowban, flag, or restrict lawful medical, nutritional, or scientific discourse."

**Provision 2: Severe Penalties for Bureaucracy Violators**
- **Immediate Termination:** Any federal employee who contacts a tech company to censor scientific debate faces immediate termination without administrative pay.
- **Loss of Federal Pension:** Bureaucrats who engage in systemic coercion forfeit their government pensions. (Watch how fast `@fda.gov` officials stop emailing tech platforms when their retirement fund is on the line).
- **Personal Civil Liability (Stripping Qualified Immunity):** Victims of government-directed medical censorship gain a direct civil right of action (*Bivens*-style remedy) against individual federal employees who coordinated their censorship.

### B. The Sunlight & Transparency Mandate (Public Censorship Log)
If a federal agency *must* communicate with a tech company regarding public health emergencies:
1. **Real-Time Disclosure:** All communications (emails, phone call transcripts, Slack messages, API flags) between federal officials and tech companies must be automatically logged and published on a publicly searchable database (`CensorshipWatch.gov`) within 24 hours.
2. **No Secret Portals:** Federal agencies are legally barred from using private portal software (like the backdoors used during 2020–2022) to submit bulk suppression requests.

### C. Section 230 Stripping for Coerced Platforms
Amending Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act:
- If a social media platform moderates medical speech based on direct government input, guidance, or coordination, **it loses Section 230 immunity** for that content. 
- Tech companies will suddenly *refuse* to talk to federal agencies because accepting a "friendly hint" from the CDC makes them legally liable in court!
