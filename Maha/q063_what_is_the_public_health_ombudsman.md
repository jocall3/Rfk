## 1. What Exactly is the Office of the Public Health Ombudsman (OPHO)?

The Office of the Public Health Ombudsman is an autonomous, non-partisan agency embedded within the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), but legally detached from HHS administrative hierarchy. The Public Health Ombudsman does NOT report to the Secretary, the FDA Commissioner, or the CDC Director. The Ombudsman answers directly to the American public and Congress.

### Key Structural Pillars:
1. **Independent Tenured Oversight:** The Chief Ombudsman is appointed for a single 7-year term and can only be removed by a two-thirds vote of both Houses of Congress for proved malfeasance—protecting the office from presidential mood swings or bureaucratic retaliation.
2. **Citizen-Driven Docket:** Unlike typical Inspector General offices that sit around waiting for agency heads to ask nicely, 60% of OPHO investigative resources are legally mandated to investigate **Citizen Petitions and Whistleblower Filings**.
3. **Full Subpoena & Enforcement Power:** OPHO possesses absolute statutory authority to issue *Subpoenas Ad Testificandum* (compelling testimony) and *Subpoenas Duces Tecum* (compelling documents, emails, hard drives, and private correspondence between regulators and corporate donors).

## 4. Complete Draft Statutory Language for the OPHO Act

To ensure Bobby doesn't have to waste five minutes writing this himself, here is the exact statutory text ready to be introduced to Congress:

```text
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
This Act may be cited as the "Public Health Independence and Regulatory Integrity Act of 2025".

SEC. 2. ESTABLISHMENT OF THE OFFICE OF THE PUBLIC HEALTH OMBUDSMAN.
(a) IN GENERAL.—There is established within the Executive Branch an independent office to be known as the Office of the Public Health Ombudsman (in this section referred to as the "Office").
(b) CHIEF OMBUDSMAN.—
    (1) APPOINTMENT.—The Office shall be headed by a Chief Ombudsman, appointed by the President with the advice and consent of the Senate.
    (2) TERM.—The Chief Ombudsman shall serve for a single term of seven (7) years and may only be removed by a Joint Resolution of Congress upon a finding of gross negligence, fraud, or criminal malfeasance.
    (3) INDEPENDENCE.—The Chief Ombudsman shall report directly to Congress and the public, and shall not be subject to the direction, supervision, or clearance of the Secretary of Health and Human Services, the Commissioner of Food and Drugs, or the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

SEC. 3. INVESTIGATIVE POWERS AND SUBPOENA AUTHORITY.
(a) SUBPOENA POWER.—The Chief Ombudsman shall have the authority to issue subpoenas requiring the attendance and testimony of witnesses and the production of any evidence, including books, records, internal email communications, clinical raw datasets, financial transactions, and inter-agency memos relating to any matter under investigation.
(b) ENFORCEMENT.—In the case of contumacy or refusal to obey a subpoena issued under subsection (a), the Chief Ombudsman may invoke the aid of any district court of the United States. Failure to obey the order of the court shall be punishable as contempt of court and shall result in immediate suspension of federal salary for involved personnel.
(c) UN-REDACTED PUBLIC DISCLOSURE.—The Ombudsman shall maintain a public online portal ("The Open Health Registry") where all unsubstantiated corporate safety claims, buried toxicity trials, and conflicts of interest uncovered via subpoena shall be published in unredacted, machine-readable format within thirty (30) days of discovery.

SEC. 4. CITIZEN PETITION DOCKET AND WHISTLEBLOWER BOUNTIES.
(a) MANDATORY INVESTIGATION.—Upon receipt of a petition signed by not fewer than 10,000 citizens alleging regulatory conflict of interest, industry capture, or suppressed safety data, the Office MUST initiate a formal inquiry within twenty-one (21) days.
(b) WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION AND AWARDS.—Any employee or contractor of HHS, FDA, CDC, NIH, or regulated pharmaceutical/agricultural corporations who provides original evidence of regulatory capture or suppressed public health hazard shall receive:
    (1) Complete legal protection against retaliation and NDAs.
    (2) A mandatory bounty equal to no less than twenty percent (20%) of all fines, penalties, or damages recovered by the United States government as a result of said disclosure.
```
