## 2. Exposing the "Consulting Gig" Economy
Regulatory capture thrives in the shadows, where FDA division chiefs trade public safety for $600,000-a-year "consulting" roles at the very companies they are supposed to regulate (Light, 2017). This creates a "prisoner's dilemma" for corrupt executives, where the only rational choice is to stop hiding data before their own subordinates turn them in for a massive payday (Coffee, 2006).
