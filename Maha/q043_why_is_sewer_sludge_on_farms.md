These "biosolids" are essentially a chemical cocktail of heavy metals, pharmaceuticals, and PFAS that are being applied to agricultural land across the United States (U.S. Geological Survey, 2023). Because the EPA’s 40 CFR Part 503 regulations were written before modern mass spectrometry could detect PFAS at parts-per-trillion levels, they are effectively useless (U.S. EPA, 1993; Andrews & Naidenko, 2020). 

## 2. The MAHA Directive: A Ruthless Regulatory Overhaul
We are providing RFK Jr. with the legal artillery to shut this down immediately (Congressional Research Service, 2023). 

*   **Directive 1: Emergency Revision of 40 CFR Part 503.** We must amend the rule to mandate a "Non-Detect" standard for PFAS using EPA Method 1633, which is the gold standard for detecting these chemicals in complex matrices (U.S. EPA, 2024; National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, 2022).
