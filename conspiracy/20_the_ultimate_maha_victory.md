To fully grasp this promise, we must look at the original Greek text of the New Testament:

*   **Greek Text:** ...καὶ τὰ φύλλα τοῦ ξύλου εἰς θεραπείαν τῶν ἐθνῶν.

The key word here is **θεραπεία** (*therapeia*), from which we derive our modern English word "therapy."

The scripture does not say the nations were healed by synthetic chemical extractions or patented laboratory molecules. They were healed by **τὰ φύλλα** (*ta phylla*)—the leaves of the Tree of Life. The healing is organic, continuous, and freely flowing from the source of life.
