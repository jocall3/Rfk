In the Hebrew Bible, the word **Shova** (or *Sova*) refers to abundance, plenty, and satiety. It is the state of being fully satisfied, content, and filled with God’s good creation. 

We see this in **Genesis 41:29**:
> "הִנֵּה יָמִים בָּאִים, שָׂבָע גָּדוֹל בְּכָל-אֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם"

*Shova* is organic. It is the natural harvest of the earth. It is satisfying because it is real. When you eat real food, your body releases leptin, the hormone that tells your brain, *"You are full. Stop eating."* God designed *Shova* to have a built-in off-switch.

The Israelites were not starving. They had Manna! But they wanted *more*. They wanted variety, they wanted heavy, fatty foods, they wanted to hoard.
