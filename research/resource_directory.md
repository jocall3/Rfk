# Agricultural Research Resource Directory

This directory serves as a foundational repository for agricultural research, encompassing databases, academic journals, public records, and OSINT tools.

## 1. Global Agricultural Databases
*   **FAOSTAT (Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations):** Provides free access to food and agriculture data for over 245 countries and territories.
    *   [https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/)
*   **USDA NASS (National Agricultural Statistics Service):** Comprehensive data on U.S. agricultural production, economics, and demographics.
    *   [https://www.nass.usda.gov/](https://www.nass.usda.gov/)
*   **CGIAR Open Access Repository:** Research outputs from the 15 CGIAR research centers focused on global food security.
    *   [https://cgspace.cgiar.org/](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/)

## 2. Academic Journals & Research Portals
*   **AgEcon Search:** A free, open-access repository of full-text scholarly literature in agricultural and applied economics.
    *   [https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/](https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/)
*   **Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry (ACS):** Peer-reviewed research on the chemistry and biochemistry of agriculture and food.
    *   [https://pubs.acs.org/journal/jafcau](https://pubs.acs.org/journal/jafcau)
*   **ScienceDirect (Agriculture & Biological Sciences):** Extensive collection of peer-reviewed journals and book chapters.
    *   [https://www.sciencedirect.com/browse/journals-and-books](https://www.sciencedirect.com/browse/journals-and-books)

## 3. Public Records & Geospatial Data
*   **USGS EarthExplorer:** Access to satellite imagery, aerial photographs, and cartographic products for land-use analysis.
    *   [https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/](https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/)
*   **Copernicus Open Access Hub:** Sentinel satellite data for monitoring crop health and environmental changes.
    *   [https://scihub.copernicus.eu/](https://scihub.copernicus.eu/)
*   **USDA Web Soil Survey:** Official source for soil data and information produced by the National Cooperative Soil Survey.
    *   [https://websoilsurvey.sc.egov.usda.gov/](https://websoilsurvey.sc.egov.usda.gov/)

## 4. OSINT & Monitoring Tools
*   **Global Forest Watch:** Interactive map for monitoring forest loss and agricultural expansion in real-time.
    *   [https://www.globalforestwatch.org/](https://www.globalforestwatch.org/)
*   **MarineTraffic / VesselFinder:** Useful for tracking agricultural commodity shipments and supply chain logistics.
    *   [https://www.marinetraffic.com/](https://www.marinetraffic.com/)
*   **Wayback Machine (Internet Archive):** Essential for retrieving historical agricultural policy documents or defunct research websites.
    *   [https://archive.org/web/](https://archive.org/web/)

## 5. Research Methodology & Best Practices
*   **PRISMA Statement:** Guidelines for reporting systematic reviews and meta-analyses in agricultural research.
    *   [http://prisma-statement.org/](http://prisma-statement.org/)
*   **Zotero:** Open-source reference management software for organizing research citations and PDFs.
    *   [https://www.zotero.org/](https://www.zotero.org/)

*Note: This directory is intended to be updated as new research avenues are identified through the ongoing 20-question research cycle.*
