# Phase 2 Research Questions: Epigenetics and Modern Dietary Patterns

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the Phase 2 research agenda for investigating the bi-directional relationship between modern dietary habits and human epigenetic modifications. Building upon foundational metabolic research, these 20 questions explore how industrial food processing, altered nutrient composition, temporal eating behaviors, and environmental food contaminants modify chromatin structure, DNA methylation, and non-coding RNA expression.

---

## Theme 1: DNA Methylation and Ultra-Processed Foods

### Question 1: Hepatocyte and Adipocyte DNA Methylation Cascades
> **How do high-fructose corn syrup (HFCS) and refined dietary sugars induce global versus gene-specific DNA methylation alterations in hepatocytes and visceral adipocytes?**
* **Focus Areas:** S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) to S-adenosylhomocysteine (SAH) ratio shifts, promoter hypermethylation of lipolytic genes (e.g., *PNPLA2*), and locus-specific demethylation of lipogenic transcription factors (e.g., *SREBF1*).
* **Research Aim:** Map the timeline of epigenetic commitment to non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) and adipocyte hypertrophy under chronic fructose exposure.

### Question 2: Histone Modifications Driven by Advanced Glycation End-Products (AGEs)
> **What specific histone post-translational modifications (e.g., H3K9me3, H3K27ac) are directly induced by dietary AGEs abundant in ultra-processed, high-heat cooked foods?**
* **Focus Areas:** RAGE receptor signaling downstream pathways, recruitment of histone deacetylases (HDACs) versus histone acetyltransferases (HATs), and persistent pro-inflammatory gene expression (*TNF-alpha*, *IL-6*).
* **Research Aim:** Characterize the chromatic "memory" left by dietary AGEs in vascular endothelial cells and circulating monocytes.

### Question 3: Xenobiotic Packaging Contaminants as Epigenetic Mutagens
> **How do dietary microplastics, phthalates, and bisphenols (BPA/BPS) leached from modern food packaging act as epigenetic disruptors when co-ingested with high-fat, high-sugar diets?**
* **Focus Areas:** Nuclear receptor interactions (PPARγ, ERα), alterations in DNA methyltransferase (DNMT1, DNMT3a, DNMT3b) expression, and altered microRNA profiles in intestinal epithelial cells.
* **Research Aim:** Disentangle the synergetic metabolic disruption caused by synthetic packaging compounds combined with lipid-dense modern diets.

### Question 4: Synthetic Food Additives and Gut Microbial Epigenetic Output
> **What is the mechanism by which synthetic emulsifiers (e.g., carboxymethylcellulose, polysorbate-80) and artificial sweeteners alter gut microbial production of short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) and subsequent host histone acetylation?**
* **Focus Areas:** Microbial butyrate and acetate depletion, host HDAC inhibition dynamics, mucosal barrier integrity gene loci (*MUC2*, *TJP1*), and immune cell polarization in GALT.
* **Research Aim:** Establish a causal pathway connecting processed food additive consumption to host chromatin remodeling via microbiome-derived metabolites.

## Theme 2: Micronutrient Imbalances, One-Carbon Metabolism, and Methyl Donors

### Question 5: Synthetic Folate Fortification versus Natural Methyl Donors
> **How does chronic high intake of synthetic folic acid from fortified foods, compared to natural dietary folates and choline, alter one-carbon flux and genomic methylation fidelity?**
* **Focus Areas:** Unmetabolized folic acid (UMFA) accumulation, dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR) saturation, MTHFR expression dynamics, and aberrant DNA methylation patterns in peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs).
* **Research Aim:** Evaluate whether hyper-fortification in industrial diets causes pseudo-methyl deficiency or non-physiological hypermethylation across key metabolic loci.

### Question 6: Paternal Dietary Methyl Donor Precursors and Gametic Epigenetics
> **What are the transgenerational epigenetic consequences of paternal diets deficient in methyl donor precursors (B12, choline, folate) combined with high saturated fat intake?**
* **Focus Areas:** Sperm small non-coding RNA (sncRNA) profiling, differential DNA methylation in paternal germline chromatin, and offspring metabolic phenotypes (insulin resistance, hepatic steatosis).
* **Research Aim:** Identify specific epigenetic marks transmitted through the paternal lineage resulting from modern Western paternal nutrition.

### Question 7: Chronic High-Sodium Consumption and Renal Chromatin Remodeling
> **How does chronic high-sodium consumption, typical of modern processed diets, alter chromatin accessibility and histone modifications in renal tubule and vascular smooth muscle cells?**
* **Focus Areas:** Salt-sensitive kinase signaling (SGK1), histone methylation at hyper-responsive sodium transporter genes (*SCNN1A*), and p300/CBP histone acetyltransferase recruitment.
* **Research Aim:** Elucidate the epigenetic infrastructure maintaining persistent salt-sensitive hypertension even after dietary sodium reduction.

## Theme 3: Non-Coding RNAs and Nutrient-Sensing Pathways

### Question 8: Dietary Exosomes and Exogenous MicroRNA Integration
> **To what extent do xenomiRs (exogenous plant and animal microRNAs) derived from processed versus fresh foods survive human digestion and regulate host metabolic gene networks?**
* **Focus Areas:** Endosomal transport, miR-156/miR-29 cross-kingdom regulation, stability of microRNAs in ultra-processed vs. raw matrices, and liver/gut target gene repression.
* **Research Aim:** Resolve the scientific debate surrounding the bioavailability and functional epigenetic potency of ingested dietary non-coding RNAs.

### Question 9: Fasting Dynamics and Long Non-Coding RNAs (lncRNAs) in Nutrient Sensing
> **How do intermittent fasting protocols versus continuous high-calorie Western dietary patterns differentially regulate lncRNAs governing autophagy and mTORC1 signaling?**
* **Focus Areas:** LncRNA-SIRT1 interactions, chromatin scaffolding by lncRNAs (e.g., *GAS5*, *MALAT1*), and hepatic gluconeogenic gene activation during nutrient scarcity vs. abundance.
* **Research Aim:** Identify key lncRNAs that switch between catabolic and anabolic chromatin architectures in response to temporal dietary cues.

### Question 10: Polyphenol-Mediated Epigenetic Reversal of Metabolic Silencing
> **What are the precise molecular kinetics by which bioactive food compounds (e.g., sulforaphane, epigallocatechin gallate, resveratrol) demethylate tumor suppressor and metabolic regulator genes silenced by modern diets?**
* **Focus Areas:** Direct DNMT inhibition, HDAC class I/II catalytic site occupancy, TET methylcytosine dioxygenase activation, and locus-specific re-expression of *PPARGC1A* and *CDKN2A*.
* **Research Aim:** Map the therapeutic potency of specific dietary bioactives to reverse Western diet-induced epigenetic silencing.

## Theme 4: Chrono-Nutrition, Circadian Clocks, and Epigenetic Oscillations

### Question 11: Circadian Misalignment via Late-Night Caloric Intake
> **How does late-night calorie consumption alter the rhythmic recruitment of histone acetyltransferases (HATs) and deacetylases (HDAC3, SIRT1) to core circadian clock gene promoters (*BMAL1*, *CLOCK*, *PER2*)?**
* **Focus Areas:** Peripheral liver and muscle clock desynchronization, loss of diurnal chromatin accessibility cycles, and dampening of metabolic gene expression oscillations.
* **Research Aim:** Quantify the degree to which temporal eating patterns override peripheral tissue epigenetic clock rhythms independent of caloric volume.

### Question 12: Epigenetic Signatures of Shift-Work Dietary Patterns
> **What distinct genome-wide epigenetic alterations are generated by non-traditional meal timing combined with high-glycemic modern diets in shift workers?**
* **Focus Areas:** Accelerated epigenetic age estimators (Horvath, GrimAge clocks), altered DNA methylation in circadian-metabolic crosstalk genes (*CRY1*, *REV-ERBα*), and systemic inflammatory markers.
* **Research Aim:** Establish diagnostic epigenetic markers for shift-work-induced metabolic syndrome to inform targeted chrono-nutritional interventions.

### Question 13: High-Fat Diet-Induced Disruption of Hepatic Chromatin Oscillations
> **How does continuous exposure to high-fat, high-saturated-fat diets alter the topological chromatin domain (TAD) architecture governing hepatic lipid and glucose metabolism?**
* **Focus Areas:** Hi-C chromatin conformation alterations, enhancer-promoter looping frequency at lipid synthesis loci, CTCF insulator binding disruption, and loss of circadian chromatin looping.
* **Research Aim:** Map 3D genome structural changes caused by chronic lipid excess and evaluate their reversibility through dietary modification.

## Theme 5: Transgenerational and Developmental Origins (DOHaD)

### Question 14: Maternal Ultra-Processed Food Consumption and Fetal Epigenetic Programming
> **What locus-specific DNA methylation profiles are imprinted on fetal umbilical cord stem cells and placenta following maternal adherence to an ultra-processed dietary pattern during pregnancy?**
* **Focus Areas:** Imprinted gene loci (*IGF2/H19*, *PLAGL1*), neurodevelopmental gene methylation, placental nutrient transporter gene regulation, and childhood adiposity risk signatures.
* **Research Aim:** Identify early-life epigenetic biomarkers predictive of childhood metabolic dysfunction resulting from maternal processed food intake.

### Question 15: Reversibility of Fetal Epigenetic Imprinting via Post-Natal Nutrition
> **Can post-natal administration of polyphenol-rich, whole-food dietary patterns reverse or mitigate unfavorable epigenetic marks established in utero due to maternal obesity or poor maternal diet?**
* **Focus Areas:** Developmental windows of epigenetic plasticity, histone methyltransferase dynamics during early childhood, histone modification reset in skeletal muscle and liver tissues.
* **Research Aim:** Determine the critical physiological windows during which post-natal dietary intervention can effectively reprogram adverse fetal epigenetic signatures.

### Question 16: Breast Milk vs. Modern Formula: Differential Epigenetic Programming
> **How do human milk oligosaccharides (HMOs) and microRNAs present in breast milk, compared to synthetic infant formulas, differentially program the epigenetic state of intestinal mucosal immune cells?**
* **Focus Areas:** DNA methylation of regulatory T-cell loci (*FOXP3*), gut epithelial maturation pathways, immune tolerance induction, and long-term inflammatory bowel disease risk reduction.
* **Research Aim:** Decipher the epigenetic mechanisms by which natural infant nutrition confers lifelong protection against autoimmune and metabolic pathology.

## Theme 6: Precision Epigenomics, Bioactive Compounds, and Future Therapeutics

### Question 17: Epigenetic Clock Acceleration and Modern Dietary Indices
> **To what degree do standardized dietary scores (e.g., Mediterranean Diet Score vs. Ultra-Processed Food Consumption Index) correlate with tissue-specific epigenetic age acceleration across diverse human populations?**
* **Focus Areas:** PhenoAge, GrimAge, and DunedinPACE metrics; cell-type composition deconvolution; oxidative stress-driven DNA methylation changes; longitudinal telomere length cross-talk.
* **Research Aim:** Validate epigenetic clocks as real-time surrogate endpoints for assessing the biological age impacts of long-term dietary patterns.

### Question 18: Ketone Bodies as Endogenous Chromatin Modifiers
> **What are the precise chromatin-modifying mechanisms of beta-hydroxybutyrate (βHB) acting as an endogenous HDAC inhibitor and histone lysine beta-hydroxybutyrylation (Kbhb) donor during ketogenic dietary regimes?**
* **Focus Areas:** Class I HDAC inhibition kinetics, global vs. promoter-specific H3K9bhb and H3K4me3 marks, starvation-response gene networks, and oxidative stress resistance pathways.
* **Research Aim:** Delineate the direct chromatin signaling role of ketone bodies beyond their metabolic function as energetic substrates.

### Question 19: Gene-Diet Epigenetic Susceptibility Loci
> **Which common single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) in epigenetic machinery genes (e.g., *DNMT3A*, *TET2*, *SIRT1*, *HDAC2*) alter an individual's epigenetic vulnerability to modern Western diets?**
* **Focus Areas:** Epigenome-wide association study (EWAS) interactions, SNP-dependent CpG methylation susceptibility, personalized nutrient-epigenetic responsiveness, and polygenic risk modification.
* **Research Aim:** Identify key genetic variations that govern individual sensitivity to diet-induced epigenetic dysregulation.

### Question 20: Multi-Omic Integration for Personalized Epigenetic Nutrition
> **How can integrated multi-omic models (combining targeted methylome sequencing, single-cell RNA-seq, and untargeted metabolomics) be operationalized to generate personalized dietary interventions for reversing metabolic disease?**
* **Focus Areas:** Machine learning integration of methylome-metabolome pathways, targeted dietary prescription algorithms, therapeutic feedback loops using liquid-biopsy epigenetic markers, and clinical trial validation frameworks.
* **Research Aim:** Establish a practical methodology for translating multi-omic data into actionable, personalized dietary protocols designed to optimize the epigenome.
