# Phase 3: Impact of Soil Health and Regenerative Agriculture on Human Metabolic Markers

This document outlines 20 research questions designed to bridge the gap between soil microbiome diversity, regenerative agricultural practices, and human metabolic health outcomes.

1. How does the concentration of soil-derived phytonutrients in regenerative crops correlate with serum antioxidant capacity in human subjects?
2. To what extent does the consumption of produce grown in high-fungal-to-bacterial ratio soils influence human gut microbiome diversity?
3. Is there a measurable difference in systemic inflammation markers (e.g., hs-CRP, IL-6) between populations consuming regenerative vs. conventional produce over a 12-month period?
4. How do soil-sequestered minerals (specifically selenium, zinc, and magnesium) in regenerative systems impact insulin sensitivity in pre-diabetic cohorts?
5. Does the presence of specific soil-borne microbes (e.g., *Mycobacterium vaccae*) in the food chain influence the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis and cortisol regulation?
6. What is the relationship between soil organic matter (SOM) levels and the density of secondary metabolites (polyphenols, flavonoids) in leafy greens?
7. Can regenerative agricultural practices reduce the bioaccumulation of heavy metals in crops, and what is the subsequent impact on human heavy metal body burden?
8. How does the fatty acid profile of livestock raised on regenerative, multi-species pastures compare to grain-fed counterparts, and what are the implications for human lipid panels?
9. Does the reduction of synthetic pesticide residues in regenerative food systems correlate with improved metabolic clearance rates in human liver function tests?
10. What role does soil-derived humic and fulvic acid intake play in human gut barrier integrity and the prevention of metabolic endotoxemia?
11. How does the "nutrient density" of regenerative crops affect satiety signaling hormones (leptin and ghrelin) compared to conventional crops?
12. Is there a correlation between the microbial diversity of the soil in which a crop is grown and the diversity of the human oral microbiome?
13. How do regenerative farming practices influence the levels of glyphosate residues in food, and what is the impact of these residues on the human shikimate pathway?
14. Can the consumption of regenerative produce improve mitochondrial efficiency as measured by oxygen consumption rates in human peripheral blood mononuclear cells?
15. What is the impact of soil-health-optimized diets on the expression of genes related to metabolic syndrome and obesity?
16. How does the soil-to-human transfer of trace elements in regenerative systems affect thyroid hormone synthesis and metabolic rate?
17. Are there specific soil-health indicators (e.g., glomalin levels) that serve as reliable proxies for the nutritional quality of the resulting food supply?
18. How does the consumption of regenerative, pasture-raised animal products influence the omega-3 to omega-6 ratio in human cell membranes?
19. Does the increased microbial complexity of regenerative soils lead to a higher intake of beneficial soil-derived probiotics in the human diet?
20. What longitudinal metabolic health outcomes are observed in communities that transition from conventional to regenerative food sourcing?
