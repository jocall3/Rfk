# Corporate Lobbying Database: Food Conglomerates & Regulatory Loopholes

This document tracks the lobbying expenditures of major food conglomerates specifically targeting regulatory loopholes related to food safety, labeling, and supply chain transparency.

## 1. Nestlé S.A.
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** FDA "Generally Recognized as Safe" (GRAS) self-determination loophole.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $2,500,000 - $3,200,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Allows companies to bypass formal FDA safety reviews for new food additives by declaring them "safe" internally.

## 2. PepsiCo, Inc.
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Sugar-Sweetened Beverage (SSB) tax exemptions and labeling requirements.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $3,500,000 - $4,000,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Exploiting definitions of "natural flavors" to avoid disclosing specific chemical compositions of proprietary additives.

## 3. The Coca-Cola Company
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Plastic waste regulation and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) loopholes.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $4,200,000 - $5,000,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Lobbying for voluntary recycling targets to preempt mandatory federal plastic reduction legislation.

## 4. Kraft Heinz
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Nutritional labeling and "Healthy" claim definitions.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $1,800,000 - $2,200,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Utilizing the "serving size" loophole to manipulate nutritional data (e.g., sugar/fat content) to meet marketing thresholds.

## 5. Tyson Foods
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** USDA inspection standards and "Product of USA" labeling.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $2,800,000 - $3,500,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Exploiting the "Product of USA" label to include meat processed in the US but raised/slaughtered abroad.

## 6. General Mills
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Bioengineered (GMO) food disclosure standards.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $1,500,000 - $2,000,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Pushing for QR-code-only disclosure methods to limit consumer access to GMO information at the point of sale.

## 7. Mondelez International
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Supply chain transparency and child labor reporting requirements.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $1,200,000 - $1,600,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Lobbying for "industry-led" certification programs that lack independent third-party verification.

## 8. Conagra Brands
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Sodium reduction guidelines and voluntary compliance frameworks.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $900,000 - $1,200,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Delaying mandatory sodium limits by promoting voluntary industry-wide reduction targets that are rarely met.

## 9. JBS S.A.
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Environmental impact reporting and deforestation supply chain audits.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $2,000,000 - $2,500,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Utilizing "indirect supplier" loopholes to avoid accountability for cattle sourced from illegally deforested land.

## 10. Kellogg Company
*   **Primary Lobbying Focus:** Front-of-package (FOP) labeling systems.
*   **Estimated Annual Expenditure (US):** $1,100,000 - $1,500,000
*   **Targeted Loopholes:** Advocating for proprietary "Facts Up Front" labeling that emphasizes positive nutrients while obscuring high sugar/sodium content.
