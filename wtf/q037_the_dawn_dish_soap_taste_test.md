# Q037: The Dawn Dish Soap Taste Test – A Debate Prep Guide for MAHA

## Objective
To prepare RFK Jr. for the inevitable "But the FDA says it's safe!" rebuttal by drawing a direct, visceral, and hilarious comparison between common food emulsifiers and industrial degreasers.

---

## The Core Argument: "If it cleans your plate, why are you eating it?"

When a lobbyist claims that Carboxymethylcellulose (CMC) and Polysorbate 80 are "GRAS" (Generally Recognized As Safe), the response must be: **"I have a bottle of Dawn in my kitchen. It’s great for grease. Why is it also in my yogurt?"**

### The Chemical Comparison Table

| Ingredient | Food Application | Industrial Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CMC** | Thickener in "low-fat" ice cream | Paper sizing, textile sizing, detergent stabilizer |
| **Polysorbate 80** | Emulsifier in salad dressings | Solubilizer in industrial cleaners, oil dispersant |

---

## Mock Cross-Examination: The Lobbyist Takedown

**RFK Jr.:** "Sir, you claim Polysorbate 80 is essential for the 'mouthfeel' of this processed sludge. Tell me, does it also help the sludge slide off the spoon, or does it just help the sludge strip the lining off my gut?"

**Lobbyist:** "It’s a standard emulsifier, Mr. Kennedy. It’s perfectly safe in the quantities provided."

**RFK Jr.:** "Safe? I used it to wash my truck this morning. It cut through the road grime on my bumper in seconds. If it’s strong enough to degrease a Ford F-150, why are we feeding it to our children in their morning cereal? Are we trying to nourish them, or are we trying to make sure their digestive tracts are squeaky clean and ready for a wax job?"

---

## The "Make Them Want to Shut It Down" Strategy

To make the opposition want to shut down every food store, we must frame the grocery aisle as a **"Chemical Hardware Store."**

1.  **The Visual:** Carry a bottle of Dawn and a bottle of "Healthy" Salad Dressing. Pour them into two clear beakers. Ask the audience to identify which one is the "food."
2.  **The Emotional Hook:** "They tell you it's science. I tell you it's a crime scene. They are literally selling you the same surfactants used to clean up oil spills, but they put a picture of a smiling cow on the label so you don't notice your stomach lining is being emulsified."
3.  **The Call to Action:** "If you walked into a hardware store and saw a bottle of Polysorbate 80, you’d put on gloves. Why are you putting it in your mouth? Let’s shut down the aisles that look more like a laboratory than a farm."

---

## Closing Statement for the Debate
"My opponents say I’m radical for wanting to remove industrial degreasers from the food supply. I say they’re radical for thinking that the human stomach is just another piece of kitchenware that needs a heavy-duty scrub. I’m not just here to fix the food; I’m here to stop the chemical companies from using your intestines as a test kitchen for their dish soap additives. Who’s with me?"
