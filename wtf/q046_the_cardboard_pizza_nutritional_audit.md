## THE "DIRT TO DUST" PIPELINE: HOW WE KILLED THE SOIL

Why are these plants so empty? Because the soil is dead. 

Industrial agriculture treats soil like dirt—literally just a physical medium to hold the plant upright while they inject it with chemical IV drips of **NPK (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium)**. 

NPK is the plant equivalent of feeding a child nothing but Red Bull and white sugar. It makes them grow incredibly fast and look big, but it completely bypasses the soil's natural microbiome. 
*   In healthy soil, plants trade carbon sugars with **mycorrhizal fungi** in exchange for trace minerals (zinc, selenium, magnesium).
*   In NPK-drenched soil, the fungi are dead. The plant is a drug addict, entirely dependent on the next chemical spray. It has no immune system, which is why it has to be sprayed with 20 different pesticides just to survive until harvest.

**If you eat plants grown in dead soil, you will become a dead person walking.**
