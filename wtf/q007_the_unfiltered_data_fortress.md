### Core Architecture of the Fortress

#### 1. The Immutable Ledger of Truth
Every time an independent study on metabolic health, pesticide toxicity, or food additives is published, it is hashed and uploaded to a decentralized, IPFS-backed database. 
* **Uncensorable:** Once a study is in the Fortress, no corrupt board of directors can vote to retract it because a sponsor got nervous.
* **Open Access:** No paywalls. No institutional logins. Just raw, unfiltered science accessible to every mom, dad, and policymaker in America.

#### 2. The Citizen Science Biomarker Pipeline
We are bypassing the slow-motion clinical trial industry by crowdsourcing real-time health data. 
* Millions of Americans who are sick of feeling like garbage can securely and anonymously upload their metabolic biomarkers (HbA1c, fasting insulin, inflammatory markers, gut microbiome sequencing) alongside their dietary habits.
* When 100,000 people cut out ultra-processed corn syrup and their systemic inflammation drops by 80% in thirty days, the dashboard displays this in real-time. You can't lobby away a dataset of a million self-reporting citizens.

#### 3. The "Seed Oil & Sludge" Correlation Engine
An automated AI engine that cross-references food additive approvals with regional chronic disease spikes. 
* If a specific emulsifier is approved for use in bread, and six months later bowel disease diagnoses spike in the zip codes consuming it, the dashboard lights up red. 
* It gives Bobby the exact ammunition he needs to walk into a press conference, point to a screen, and say: *"Explain this."*
