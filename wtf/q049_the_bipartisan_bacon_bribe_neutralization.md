## The Counter-Measure: The Pasture-Raised Pork Truth Bomb
To neutralize the bipartisan bacon bribe, we must deploy the **Pasture-Raised Pork Truth Bomb**. This is a two-pronged strategy of aggressive education and superior culinary alternatives.

### Step 1: The Sensory Intervention
You cannot defeat bacon with a lecture on kale. You must defeat bad bacon with *superior* bacon. 
* **The Weapon:** 100% pasture-raised, heritage-breed pork (such as Berkshire or Duroc), cured using only sea salt and celery powder (or completely uncured), smoked over real hickory wood.
* **The Execution:** Set up a mobile cooking station directly outside the committee room. The aroma of actual, nutrient-dense fat rendering from pigs that actually ran around in the sunlight will completely overpower the chemical smell of the lobbyist's factory bacon. 
* **The Psychological Effect:** When politicians taste real pasture-raised pork, their brains will register actual nutrition (high in Vitamin D and healthy monounsaturated fats). They will realize they have been eating cardboard and chemicals for the last ten years. They will look at the lobbyist's plate with disgust.

### Step 2: The Legislative "Nitrite Check"
We must introduce a mandatory disclosure rule for all food served on Capitol Hill:
* **The "Pink Meat" Disclosure:** Any meat containing synthetic sodium nitrite served within congressional offices must carry a warning label: *"Consuming this bacon may impair your ability to vote rationally on agricultural policy."*
* **The Lobbyist Expense Audit:** Force lobbyists to disclose the exact sourcing of their breakfast spreads. If they cannot prove their bacon lived a happy life on pasture, it is banned from the building.
