## What is PDUFA? (Or: How to Buy a Regulator)

Back in 1992, the FDA was "slow." Drug companies complained that it took too long to get their chemical concoctions approved. Instead of telling them to go pound sand and do better science, Congress passed the **Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA)**. 

The pitch was simple: *“Hey, what if the drug companies paid the FDA a 'user fee' to speed up the review process?”*

Think about this for three seconds. If you are a health inspector, and the local rat-infested restaurant pays 75% of your salary directly to speed up their inspection, are you going to shut them down? Or are you going to look at a kitchen floor covered in rodent feces and say, *"Wow, look at that rustic, organic seasoning!"*?

Today, **nearly 75% of the FDA’s budget for scientific reviews of human drugs comes directly from the pharmaceutical industry.** 

The FDA is not a government watchdog. It is a security guard hired by Big Pharma, wearing a badge made of tin foil.
