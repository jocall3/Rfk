## THE DECONTAMINATION PROTOCOL (STEP-BY-STEP)

### Step 1: The Hazmat Sweep
Equip yourself with a large black trash bag. Walk into your kitchen or school cafeteria. If you see a box of cereal that looks like a neon sign in Las Vegas, do not hesitate. Do not look at the price tag. Do not listen to the children weeping in the background. They will thank you when their attention spans last longer than four seconds. 
*Rule of thumb: If the food does not exist in nature in that color, it should not exist in your digestive tract.*

### Step 2: Read the Fine Print
The enemy is sneaky. They won't always label it "Petroleum Red." Look for these aliases on the ingredient list:
*   **FD&C Red No. 40** (Allura Red AC)
*   **FD&C Yellow No. 5** (Tartrazine)
*   **FD&C Yellow No. 6** (Sunset Yellow FCF)
*   **FD&C Blue No. 1** (Brilliant Blue FCF)
*   **FD&C Blue No. 2** (Indigo Carmine)

If you see any of these, the item is classified as "Hazardous Waste" and must be disposed of immediately.

### Step 3: The "Real Food" Re-Education
Explain to the children (and the cafeteria staff) that apples do not require a warning label in Europe, whereas American foods containing these dyes literally have to carry warning labels in other countries saying they "may have an adverse effect on activity and attention in children." We are the only ones crazy enough to treat Red 40 like a basic food group.

You do not have to feed your kids cardboard to keep them healthy. Nature actually figured out colors millions of years before chemists in lab coats decided to see what happened when you synthesize coal tar.

### 1. The "Actually Yellow" Mac & Cheese
*   **The Old Way:** Boxed mac with a powder packet that glows in the dark.
*   **The New Way:** Real pasta, real butter, real cheddar cheese, and a pinch of **turmeric** or **paprika** for that classic golden hue. It tastes like actual food because it *is* actual food.

### 2. The "Real Berry" Red Slushie
*   **The Old Way:** Red 40 syrup mixed with frozen water.
*   **The New Way:** Blended frozen strawberries, raspberries, a splash of pomegranate juice, and a touch of honey. It’s sweet, it’s red, and it won't make your kids try to climb the drywall.

### 3. The "Blue Sky" Smoothie

*   **The New Way:** Blueberries, bananas, and a pinch of **blue spirulina** (a natural algae that provides a stunning blue color without the behavioral side effects of industrial dye).
