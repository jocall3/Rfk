# DEPOSITION TRANSCRIPT: THE "THREE GUYS NAMED GARY" (GRAS SELF-CERTIFICATION PANEL)

**DATE:** October 24, 2024
**LOCATION:** A dimly lit backroom of a "Vape & Vitamin" shop, Des Moines, IA
**SUBJECT:** Investigation into the "Generally Recognized as Safe" (GRAS) loophole for synthetic food additives.

---

**ATTORNEY:** For the record, please state your names.

**GARY 1:** Gary.
**GARY 2:** Gary.
**GARY 3:** Gary.

**ATTORNEY:** Right. And you three constitute the entirety of the "Independent Safety Review Board" for the additive known as "Hyper-Glaze 9000"?

**GARY 1:** That’s us. We’re the experts. I have a degree in interpretive dance, Gary 2 here is a retired professional mascot, and Gary 3 is a goldfish enthusiast.

**GARY 2:** (Nodding) I once ate a crayon in 1994. I’m basically a toxicologist.

**ATTORNEY:** And you were paid $400,000 by the manufacturer to determine if this chemical—which glows in the dark and melts plastic—is "Generally Recognized as Safe"?

**GARY 3:** We looked at the data. Well, we looked at the check. Then we looked at a picture of a salad. We decided it was safe.

**ATTORNEY:** Did you conduct any clinical trials?

**GARY 1:** We held a meeting at a Chili’s. We ordered the appetizers. We felt fine afterward. That’s the "Generally Recognized" part. It’s recognized by us. We are the "General."

**ATTORNEY:** But the FDA has no oversight on this process? You just email yourselves a certificate and the food hits the shelves?

**GARY 2:** Look, buddy, do you want your cereal to be neon blue or do you want to be a buzzkill? RFK Jr. is out there trying to take away our right to eat industrial lubricants disguised as breakfast, and we’re the only thing standing between him and a world where people actually know what’s in their food.

**GARY 3:** Yeah, if people knew what we approved, they’d burn every grocery store to the ground. We’re doing the public a favor by keeping them in the dark. It’s a public service!

**ATTORNEY:** You realize this chemical is linked to—

**GARY 1:** (Interrupting) Linked to *flavor*, maybe. Listen, we’re busy. We have a meeting with a company that wants to put liquid rubber in baby formula. It’s a big day.

**ATTORNEY:** This is insane. You’re literally three guys in a strip mall rubber-stamping poison.

**GARY 2:** We prefer the term "Regulatory Consultants." Now, are you going to buy a vape or are you going to keep asking questions that make us look like the villains in a dystopian thriller?

**ATTORNEY:** I’m done.

**GARY 3:** Great. Gary 1, sign the paper. Gary 2, get the stamp. I’ll go get the snacks. I think these chips are made of recycled tires, but they’re GRAS, baby!
