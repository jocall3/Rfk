### Section 3: Policy Blueprint — The GRAS Corporate Amnesty Revocation Act [4]

This Act completely dismantles the self-certification process and shifts the burden of proof back to where it belongs: on the corporations trying to feed us chemistry experiments [4].

#### Pillar 1: Immediate Revocation of Self-Certification [9]
Effective immediately, the "voluntary notification" system for GRAS substances is abolished [9]. No chemical may be added to human food unless it has undergone independent, third-party testing verified by an uncompromised regulatory body [10].

#### Pillar 2: Shifting the Burden of Proof [4]
Under the old system, the public had to prove a chemical was dangerous after it had already been in the food supply for twenty years [2]. Under this Act, the manufacturer must prove beyond a shadow of a doubt that a chemical is safe *before* a single microgram touches a grocery store shelf [4].

#### Pillar 3: The Independent Safety Review Board (ISRB) [3]
We are establishing a brand-new, completely independent board of toxicologists, pediatricians, and metabolic health experts [3]. Anyone who has received a single dollar of funding, consulting fees, or free lunches from a food processing company or chemical manufacturer in the last ten years is permanently barred from serving on this board [1]. Furthermore, board members must be willing to consume the chemical in question in front of a live studio audience before approving it [2].

#### Pillar 4: Retroactive Review of Existing GRAS Additives [10]
Every single chemical currently enjoying "GRAS" status—including artificial dyes, emulsifiers, and synthetic preservatives—will have its status suspended pending a systematic review by the ISRB over the next 24 months [10].

### Section 4: Executive Order Template (Day One Action) [10]

#### EXECUTIVE ORDER: RESTORING FOOD SAFETY AND ELIMINATING UNTESTED CHEMICAL ADDITIVES FROM THE AMERICAN DIET [4]

By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America, it is hereby ordered as follows: [4]

**Section 1. Policy.** It is the policy of this Administration to ensure that the American food supply is free from untested, hazardous, and toxic chemical additives [4]. The health of our nation's children is paramount, and the regulatory capture of food safety agencies by industrial food conglomerates must end [1].

**Section 2. Revocation of the GRAS Loophole.**  
(a) The Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the Commissioner of Food and Drugs, shall immediately cease accepting voluntary GRAS notices [9].  
(b) The FDA shall transition to a mandatory pre-market approval process for all food additives, synthetic substances, and chemical formulations intended for human consumption [4].

**Section 3. Establishment of the Independent Food Safety Task Force.**  
(a) There is hereby established an Independent Food Safety Task Force, to be chaired by the Secretary of Health and Human Services (or their designated representative, Robert F. Kennedy Jr.) [4].  
(b) The Task Force shall conduct an immediate audit of all food additives currently classified as "Generally Recognized as Safe" and compile a "Red List" of substances to be banned within 90 days, including but not limited to Titanium Dioxide, Potassium Bromate, Azodicarbonamide, BHA, BHT, and Artificial Food Dyes [7].

**Section 4. Enforcement.** Any corporation found to be introducing unapproved chemical additives into the interstate food supply after the date of this order shall face immediate seizure of products, heavy financial penalties, and potential criminal prosecution of executive officers [10].
