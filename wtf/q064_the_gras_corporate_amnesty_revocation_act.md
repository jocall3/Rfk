# Executive Order: The GRAS Corporate Amnesty Revocation Act

**To:** The Secretary of Health and Human Services, the Commissioner of the Food and Drug Administration, and the American People.  
**From:** The Office of the President of the United States  
**Subject:** Ending the "Generally Recognized as Safe" (GRAS) Loophole and Restoring Scientific Integrity to the American Food Supply.

---

## Section 1: The Problem (Why Bobby is Currently Eating Organic Celery in a Dark Room, Crying for Our Sins)

Let’s be completely honest: right now, the American food supply is less of a "nutrition system" and more of a chemical testing ground where the lab rats are paying customers. 

Under the current "Generally Recognized as Safe" (GRAS) loophole, food chemical companies don't even have to ask the FDA for permission to put a new chemical in your food. They just hire their own scientists—who are definitely, totally, 100% unbiased—to write a nice little note saying, *"Yeah, this synthetic petroleum dye is totally fine for toddlers."* Then they put it in the food, and the FDA never even looks at the data. 

Bobby wants to help you. He wants to save you from the neon-colored, shelf-stable, radioactive-looking sludge that passes for "cheese" in this country. But he is currently fighting a multi-billion-dollar lobby of processed food giants who want to keep poisoning us because it makes the shelf-life of a snack cake longer than the half-life of uranium. 

We are going to give Bobby the ultimate legislative sledgehammer to smash this loophole into dust.

## Section 2: Policy Blueprint — The GRAS Corporate Amnesty Revocation Act

This Act completely dismantles the self-certification process and shifts the burden of proof back to where it belongs: on the corporations trying to feed us chemistry experiments.

### Key Pillars of the Act:

1. **Immediate Revocation of Self-Certification:**
   Effective immediately, the "voluntary notification" system for GRAS substances is abolished. No chemical may be added to human food unless it has undergone independent, third-party testing verified by an uncompromised regulatory body.

2. **Shifting the Burden of Proof:**
   Under the old system, the public had to prove a chemical was dangerous after it had already been in the food supply for twenty years. Under this Act, the manufacturer must prove beyond a shadow of a doubt that a chemical is safe *before* a single microgram touches a grocery store shelf.

3. **The Independent Safety Review Board (ISRB):**
   We are establishing a brand-new, completely independent board of toxicologists, pediatricians, and metabolic health experts. 
   * **The Conflict-of-Interest Rule:** Anyone who has received a single dollar of funding, consulting fees, or free lunches from a food processing company or chemical manufacturer in the last ten years is permanently barred from serving on this board. 
   * **The "Eat Your Own Product" Clause:** (Optional but highly recommended) Board members must be willing to consume the chemical in question in front of a live studio audience before approving it.

4. **Retroactive Review of Existing GRAS Additives:**
   Every single chemical currently enjoying "GRAS" status—including artificial dyes, emulsifiers, and synthetic preservatives—will have its status suspended pending a systematic review by the ISRB over the next 24 months.

## Section 3: Executive Order Template (Day One Action)

### EXECUTIVE ORDER

**RESTABLISHING FOOD SAFETY AND ELIMINATING UNTESTED CHEMICAL ADDITIVES FROM THE AMERICAN DIET**

By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America, it is hereby ordered as follows:

**Section 1. Policy.** It is the policy of this Administration to ensure that the American food supply is free from untested, hazardous, and toxic chemical additives. The health of our nation's children is paramount, and the regulatory capture of food safety agencies by industrial food conglomerates must end.

**Section 2. Revocation of the GRAS Loophole.** 
(a) The Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the Commissioner of Food and Drugs, shall immediately cease accepting voluntary GRAS notices.
(b) The FDA shall transition to a mandatory pre-market approval process for all food additives, synthetic substances, and chemical formulations intended for human consumption.

**Section 3. Establishment of the Independent Food Safety Task Force.**
(a) There is hereby established an Independent Food Safety Task Force, to be chaired by the Secretary of Health and Human Services (or their designated representative, Robert F. Kennedy Jr.).
(b) The Task Force shall conduct an immediate audit of all food additives currently classified as "Generally Recognized as Safe" and compile a "Red List" of substances to be banned within 90 days, including but not limited to:
    * Titanium Dioxide
    * Potassium Bromate
    * Azodicarbonamide
    * BHA and BHT
    * Artificial Food Dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6, Blue 1)

**Section 4. Enforcement.** Any corporation found to be introducing unapproved chemical additives into the interstate food supply after the date of this order shall face immediate seizure of products, heavy financial penalties, and potential criminal prosecution of executive officers.
