### THE ENEMY: THE "STABILITY-AGENTS" (THE LAZY CORPORATE SLIME)

What are they actually putting in your food to keep it from separating? Let’s look at the rogue’s gallery of corporate laziness:

1. **Carrageenan:** Literally seaweed processed with harsh chemicals (often including potassium hydroxide) to create a gel that keeps your "almond milk" from looking like the watery nut-juice it actually is. It’s known to cause gut inflammation so severe that scientists literally use it to induce colitis in lab rats when they want to test anti-inflammatory drugs. Yes, you are eating the rat-colitis-starter-pack so your fake milk looks "creamy."
2. **Xanthan Gum:** A slimy exopolysaccharide produced by the fermentation of simple sugars by the plant-pathogenic bacterium *Xanthomonas campestris*. It’s the same bacteria that causes black rot on broccoli. They harvest the slime, dry it into a powder, and dump it into your salad dressing so the oil and vinegar don't have to look at each other.
3. **Polysorbate 80:** An industrial surfactant. A detergent. It’s used in cosmetics, vaccines, and... your ice cream. It keeps the ice cream from melting too fast and keeps the fat from separating. It also strips the protective mucosal lining right off your intestinal wall, leaving your gut barrier as thin and useless as a wet single-ply napkin at a cheap diner.
4. **Mono- and Diglycerides:** The ultimate loophole. These are byproducts of oil processing that contain trans fats, but because they are classified as *emulsifiers* and not *lipids*, food companies can print "0g Trans Fat" on the label while filling the product with heart-clogging sludge.
