Below is a comprehensive, **100-part interrogation and policy blueprint framework** designed to drive a deep-dive research project on the **"Make America Healthy Again" (MAHA)** movement led by HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.

This framework breaks down the agenda across 10 core administrative, scientific, and legal domains to systematically analyze, formalize, and draft federal policy.

---

### **Domain I: Defining Ultra-Processed Foods (UPFs) & Agricultural Reform**

1. How should federal regulations legally define an "Ultra-Processed Food" (UPF) using the NOVA classification system without creating regulatory loopholes?
2. What specific statutory changes are required in the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act) to restrict UPF marketing to minors?
3. How can federal agricultural subsidies under the U.S. Farm Bill be shifted away from monoculture crops (corn, soy) toward diversified specialty crops?
4. What mechanisms can be written into USDA rules to incentivize regenerative farming practices for small-to-medium agricultural producers?
5. How can HHS and USDA mandate front-of-pack labeling for processing intensity and added synthetic compounds?
6. What administrative pathways exist to require immediate premarket testing for all newly introduced food formulations?
7. How should federal policy handle industrial seed oil extraction processes regarding health claims and safety reviews?
8. What regulatory incentives can be established for food manufacturers that voluntarily reformulate products away from synthetic emulsifiers?
9. How can federal purchasing guidelines for military bases, prisons, and public hospitals be rewritten to enforce whole-food procurement?
10. What metrics should be established to measure soil microbiome health as a component of federal nutritional policy?

### **Domain II: FDA & GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe) Loophole Overhaul**

11. What precise legislative draft is needed to eliminate manufacturer self-affirmation under the FDA’s GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe) loophole?
12. How can the FDA implement a mandatory retro-active safety review for the ~10,000 additives currently on the market?
13. What legal authority allows HHS to ban petroleum-based artificial food dyes (e.g., Red 40, Yellow 5) in federally supported nutrition programs?
14. How should the FDA restructure its advisory committees to systematically eliminate corporate conflicts of interest?
15. What policy mechanisms will replace FDA industry user fees (PDUFA/MDUFA) with direct, uncompromised congressional appropriations?
16. How can the FDA mandate phase-outs of endocrine-disrupting packaging materials (PFAS, phthalates, BPA) in food contact surfaces?
17. What legal framework can restrict deceptive health claims on processed foods that use synthetic fortification to mask high refined sugar content?
18. How can FDA inspection protocols be modernized to track environmental toxin residues in imported food products?
19. What policy will mandate full transparency of "natural flavors" and chemical extraction solvents on ingredient labels?
20. What statutory changes are necessary to grant the FDA expanded emergency recall authority over chemical contaminants?

### **Domain III: Federal Nutrition Programs (SNAP, WIC, School Lunches)**

21. What executive orders or USDA waiver frameworks can legally restrict SNAP/WIC benefits from purchasing sugar-sweetened beverages and ultra-processed snacks?
22. How can SNAP-Ed (SNAP Education) funds be restructured to prioritize hands-on cooking, gardening, and metabolic health literacy?
23. What policy draft is required to update National School Lunch Program (NSLP) standards to eliminate synthetic additives and high-fructose corn syrup?
24. How can federal policy expand double-up food buck programs to make organic and local produce more accessible to low-income populations?
25. What legal protections are needed to safeguard states that implement stricter food dye bans in school meals from federal funding penalties?
26. How can the federal government integrate direct farm-to-school supply chain logistics into the USDA baseline budget?
27. What criteria should determine whether a food product is exempt from or subject to federal nutrition welfare restrictions?
28. How can local food hubs be granted direct access to federal institutional purchasing contracts?
29. What policies can be introduced to mandate free, clean, fluoridated-free or purified drinking water infrastructure in public schools?
30. How can federal nutrition assistance programs track and reward measurable long-term improvements in community metabolic health markers?

### **Domain IV: Environmental Toxins, Pesticides & Water Quality**

31. What regulatory process can the EPA and HHS use to systematically re-evaluate glyphosate, atrazine, and neonicotinoids under updated toxicity standards?
32. How can federal policies establish liability frameworks for chemical runoff that damages local water systems and soil biomes?
33. What legal pathways allow HHS to issue updated national guidance on municipal water fluoridation?
34. How can the EPA’s Integrated Risk Information System (IRIS) be overhauled to accelerate toxicological assessments of industrial chemicals?
35. What federal standards should be enacted to regulate microplastic exposure and leaching in municipal tap water?
36. How can pesticide registration rules be updated to require long-term studies on human gut microbiome disruption?
37. What national policy can mandate full public disclosure of all inert ingredients used in commercial pesticide formulations?
38. How can HHS collaborate with the EPA to establish strict, enforceable limits on PFAS chemicals in agricultural biosolids?
39. What federal incentives can be created to support municipal transitions toward advanced water filtration systems?
40. How can environmental health tracking systems be integrated into national public health reporting databases?

### **Domain V: NIH Research Re-allocation & Scientific Transparency**

41. How should NIH grant allocation rules be restructured to shift funding from chronic symptom management toward root-cause lifestyle and environmental research?
42. What policy draft will cap NIH indirect cost overhead reimbursements to force universities to spend funds directly on research?
43. How can federal law mandate open-access raw data publication for all federally funded clinical trials and observational studies?
44. What framework can be established to conduct independent, gold-standard, long-term safety studies on childhood chronic disease triggers?
45. How can NIH research priorities be expanded to study the health impacts of electromagnetic field (EMF) exposures and screen-time habits?
46. What rules are necessary to ban researchers with active pharmaceutical or chemical industry consulting contracts from NIH review boards?
47. How can federal policy incentivize research into non-pharmaceutical interventions (nutritional therapy, exercise, circadian biology)?
48. What administrative changes can streamline the NIH grant application process to reward innovative, non-mainstream scientific hypotheses?
49. How should federal scientific agencies define and enforce open-source scientific replication studies?
50. What mechanisms can be put in place to protect scientific whistleblowers within HHS, FDA, and CDC from professional retaliation?

51. What administrative steps are needed to consolidate overlapping HHS divisions into a streamlined *Administration for a Healthy America*?
52. How can the CDC’s public health guidance responsibilities be legally decoupled from its commercial operations and patent-holding frameworks?
53. What policy draft is required to restructure the CDC’s Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP) to guarantee independent ethics oversight?
54. How can HHS regional offices be downsized or re-tasked to focus on direct community health and agricultural outreach?
55. What legislative framework can prohibit "revolving door" employment between HHS agencies and regulated healthcare industries?
56. How can federal agency communications be legally restricted from promoting single-source pharmaceutical solutions over lifestyle interventions?
57. What measures can be taken to audit and eliminate redundant administrative overhead across all 15 HHS sub-agencies?
58. How can HHS establish an independent Ombudsman Office specifically for patient advocacy against regulatory capture?
59. What administrative tools can enforce real-time transparency for all agency decision-making processes and public health directives?
60. How can HHS workforce incentives be aligned with measurable national reductions in chronic illness rates?

### **Domain VII: Chronic Disease Prevention & Healthcare Payment Models**

61. How can Medicare and Medicaid reimbursement rules be modified to cover medically tailored meals, nutrition counseling, and exercise prescriptions?
62. What legislative changes are needed to allow Health Savings Accounts (HSAs) and FSAs to cover gym memberships, organic food, and preventative wellness tools?
63. How can insurance coverage models be shifted from fee-for-service toward value-based outcome models tied to metabolic disease reversal?
64. What policy will mandate metabolic health screenings (fasting insulin, HbA1c, inflammatory markers) as standard covered preventative care?
65. How can federal incentives encourage healthcare systems to hire certified metabolic health and lifestyle medicine specialists?
66. What regulatory framework can prevent health insurance carriers from raising premiums on individuals utilizing alternative therapies?
67. How can Medicaid block grants be restructured to give states flexibility in funding community-based preventive health programs?
68. What federal policies can support the expansion of non-profit direct primary care (DPC) models across underserved populations?
69. How can federal employee health benefit plans be rewritten to serve as the national gold-standard model for preventative care coverage?
70. What legal standards should govern corporate wellness incentives to ensure they prioritize genuine lifestyle interventions over biometric compliance?
